Privacy Policy

Japmala for Android. Effective date: 6 November 2026

Japmala ("the app") is a naam jap and japa mala counter for Android that works offline, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your jap stays on your phone, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows banner ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them. The app also records two usage events with Firebase Analytics, under an app instance identifier that is not your name or email, and never which naam you chant.

1. Who we are

Japmala is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer in India. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your jap stays on your phone

The app saves what you do only on your phone, in the app's private storage: your counts and malas by date, entries you add by hand, where you are in each mala, your own naams and mantras, your daily goal and its history, your daily targets and sankalps, the paths you marked as read, and your settings and reminder times.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no server that receives your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • All naams and path texts are bundled inside the app, so counting and reading need no internet. The internet is used only for ads, Google's privacy message and the usage events described in sections 4, 5 and 6.

3. The backup file

When you choose "बैकअप फ़ाइल बनाएँ" (make a backup file), the app writes one file with your whole jap history: counts and malas by date, hand entries, mala positions, your own naams, your goal history, targets, the paths you read and your settings. The file is readable text (JSON), so anyone who has the file can read your jap history. It goes wherever you save or share it: the app opens Android's own file picker or share sheet, and you choose the place, for example your Downloads folder, Google Drive or another app. The app does not upload the file anywhere by itself, and we never receive it. Once the file is outside the app, the place or app you chose decides how it is kept. Keep it somewhere you trust.

When you restore from a file, the app reads only the file you pick. Before it replaces anything it keeps a copy of your current data inside the app, so you can undo the restore for up to 7 days, or until you save anything new; that copy stays on your phone and is deleted after that.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Japmala is free and supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. A banner appears only on the home screen and on the stats screen (मेरा जप), in its own strip. No ad ever appears while you count, while you read a path, during the first setup, or in a dialog, and there are no full screen or video ads. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device or other identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device, and where applicable identifiers of accounts signed in on the device
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location
  • app interactions, such as app launches and how you interact with ads, for example views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your jap history. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

We block dating, gambling, get rich and cleaner ad categories and allow ads rated PG at most. AdMob's filters cannot block every ad, so if you see an ad that should not be in a jap app, use "अनुचित विज्ञापन बताएँ" (report an inappropriate ad) in Settings; it opens your email app with a message naming the screen and the app version, and we act on reports.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how many people come back to the app after a day, a week and a month. It records only two events of its own: backup_made (a backup file was made) and restore_done (a backup file was restored). It never records which naam you chant or which path you read, your counts, malas, targets or goals. Automatic screen reporting is turned off, so screen names never reveal what you read; advertising personalisation signals are turned off; and Analytics does not collect the advertising ID. Firebase also records standard information automatically, such as app opens and sessions, the app version, Android version, device model, language, a coarse location derived from a masked IP address, and an app instance identifier that is not your name or email. Firebase Analytics is run by Google and follows Google's privacy terms at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (the User Messaging Platform consent tool) on the home screen before personalised ads or analytics storage are enabled, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. A change applies at once. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalised ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalised.

7. Location

The app never asks for location permission and never reads your phone's location. Google's ad and analytics SDKs can still estimate an approximate location, such as the city or region, from your IP address, as described in sections 4 and 5.

8. Reminders are optional

The morning and evening reminders are off until you turn them on, in Settings or when the app offers them after your first mala. They are planned and shown by your phone, and nothing about them is sent to us. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications when you turn a reminder on; you can deny it, or change it later in Android settings. Reminders never carry ads or promotions.

9. Reporting a problem

The report buttons in the app ("गलती दिखे? बताएँ" on a path, report a problem and report an ad in Settings) only open your email app with a message you can read and edit before you send it. It names the item or screen and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

10. Android backup and a new phone

The app allows Android's own backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can copy your jap data to your Google Drive backup and restore it when you set up a new phone with the same account. This is done by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot see the backup. It is a bonus, not a promise; the backup file in section 3 is the dependable way. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's Google settings.

11. Permissions

  • Vibration: for the tick on each bead and at each mala. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Notifications: only for the reminders, once you turn one on. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Internet and network state: only for ads, Firebase Analytics and Google's privacy message. Counting and reading work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or photos. It reads or writes a file only when you pick one in Android's file picker.

12. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and analytics information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

13. Age

Japmala is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

14. Deleting your data

Your jap stays on your phone until you remove it. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; either removes everything the app stored on your phone. Backup files you made stay wherever you saved them until you delete them. If Android backup is on, delete the backup from your Google account settings. Emails you sent us are deleted on request.

15. Security

Your jap stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled, and keep backup files somewhere private. The ad and analytics services (Google's SDKs) control how they send data, and Google states that they encrypt it in transit.

16. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

How do I count?

Pick a naam on the home screen and tap "जप शुरू करें". Tap anywhere on the mala ring to count one jap; the count is saved before the screen says "सहेजा गया". The control bar has −1, the haptic and bell switches, the volume keys switch, pocket mode and reset. With volume keys on, each press counts once while the counting screen is open and the phone is unlocked.

Does it need internet?

No. All naams and path texts are inside the app and there is no sign in. Only ads, Google's privacy message and two usage events use the internet.

How do I keep my jap safe, or move it to a new phone?

In Settings, open Backup and choose "बैकअप फ़ाइल बनाएँ". Save the file in Downloads or Google Drive, or share it to yourself. On the new phone, install Japmala, open Backup and choose "फ़ाइल से वापस लाएँ". The app shows the file's date and totals next to the phone's before it replaces anything, and you can undo a restore for up to 7 days, or until you save anything new. The file holds your jap history in readable form, so keep it private. Android's own Google backup may also bring your jap back on a new phone, but the file is the dependable way.

A naam or a verse looks wrong. How do I report it?

In the path reader, use "गलती दिखे? बताएँ". It opens your email app with a message that already names the verse and the app version; add what you think is wrong and send it. You can also write to anshul.jainsm@gmail.com. Confirmed errors are fixed in a later update.

Why are there ads?

Banner ads from Google AdMob keep Japmala free. They appear only on the home screen and the stats screen, never while you count or read. If an ad seems wrong for a jap app, use "अनुचित विज्ञापन बताएँ" in Settings. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

How do the reminders work?

Turn on the morning or evening reminder in Settings and pick a time. On Android 13 and later, allow notifications when the app asks. Reminders can arrive a few minutes late, and are skipped on days your goal and targets are already met.

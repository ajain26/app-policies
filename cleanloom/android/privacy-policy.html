Privacy Policy

Clean Loom for Android. Last updated: October 12, 2026

Clean Loom ("the app") is a storage cleaner for Android, published by the developer listed on its Google Play page ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos, videos, contacts and calendar events are processed on your device and never sent to us. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. What stays on your device

All cleanup work happens on your phone: finding duplicate and similar photos, screenshots and videos, compressing videos, finding duplicate and incomplete contacts, and finding old, duplicate or promotional calendar events. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no account or sign in.

  • Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Your contacts and calendar events are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

2. Photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo and video library to compare images, show thumbnails and previews, add up how much space they use, compress the videos you choose, and delete the items you select. On Android 14 and later you can allow access to selected photos only, and the app then works with those.

Deleting is always your choice. Items can wait in the in-app Safety Bin, which is cleared when the app closes, and nothing is deleted until you confirm it. A deleted item is permanently removed from your device. Clean Loom cannot bring it back, although a copy may remain in a cloud backup you use, such as Google Photos.

Videos you compress are processed on your device and saved as a new video next to the original.

3. Contacts

With your permission, the app reads your contacts to find duplicates and incomplete entries, and changes them only when you merge, edit or delete. Before every merge or delete it saves a backup of the affected contacts as a vCard file in the app's private storage, so you can restore them. Backups stay on your device until you delete them in Contact Backups or uninstall the app. A backup leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

4. Calendar

With your permission, the app reads your calendars to find old, duplicate or promotional events and deletes only the events you select. The events are checked on your device and never sent to us. If an event belongs to a synced account, such as Google Calendar, deleting it also removes it from that account on all of your devices.

5. Private Vault

Items you move into the Vault are encrypted with AES 256 using a key kept in the Android Keystore and are stored only in the app's private storage, which is excluded from backups. Your vault passcode is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android: the app never receives your biometric data. Files decrypted for viewing are deleted when you close them, and any left behind are removed the next time the app starts.

6. Advertising

Clean Loom is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information about the ads SDK, such as crash and performance data

Ads never see your photos, videos, contacts, calendar events or vault items. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Your ad choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Without consent or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show non personalized ads.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and videos: to scan, preview, compress and delete the items you choose.
  • Contacts: to find, merge, edit and delete duplicate and incomplete contacts.
  • Calendar: to find and delete old, duplicate and promotional events.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the Vault with your fingerprint or face.
  • Internet and network state: only to load ads.

Each permission is optional. You can deny or revoke it in your Android settings, and the related feature then stops working while the rest of the app keeps working.

9. What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a few things in its private storage: your settings, onboarding progress, the list of photos you marked as not duplicates, your Cleaning Guide checkmarks, lifetime cleanup totals, contact backups and Vault items. This information stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app, and it is excluded from device backups and transfers.

10. Purchases

Clean Loom has no in-app purchases, so the app never handles payment information.

11. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

12. Children

Clean Loom is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

13. Security

Because your content is processed on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled and back up the photos and contacts that matter to you.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the latest version is always available in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

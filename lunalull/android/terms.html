1. What LunaLull is

LunaLull is a sleep sound mixer: layered nature and noise sounds, three acoustic distances, a sleep timer that fades the sound out, an optional gentle wake alarm, breathing patterns, binaural beds and mixes built from a sentence by a keyword reader on your phone. It is meant for relaxation and sleep routines. It does not track your sleep.

LunaLull for Android: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

LunaLull for Android ("the app") is a sleep sound app published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: there is no account, your mixes and settings stay on your phone, every sound plays on your phone, and we run no server that stores your data. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

LunaLull for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your phone

  • Your saved mixes, layers, fader positions and acoustic distances.
  • Your favourites, your nights calendar and your morning check ins.
  • Your onboarding answers, your persona and your bedtime.
  • Your timer, alarm and notification settings.
  • Every downloaded sound file.

All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us: we cannot see, access or recover it. There is no account, login or registration. The mix you build from a sentence is put together by a keyword reader on your phone; nothing you type there leaves the phone.

LunaLull for Android contains no analytics and no crash reporting, and no Firebase SDK. We receive no usage statistics about you.

3. Sound plays on your phone

Every sound in the app is stored in the app and played on your phone. Nothing you play is streamed, recorded or sent anywhere. LunaLull never uses the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts, your photos or your files. There is no sleep tracking and no health data.

4. Optional sound downloads

The Through a Wall and Outside versions of each sound are optional downloads, fetched only when you download a sound or a pack (Settings, Downloads, or a sound's details). They come from our file hosting at lunalull.web.app, which runs on Google's Firebase Hosting. Like any website, the hosting service receives your IP address and the names of the files requested. No identifier is sent with these requests.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

LunaLull is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner inside the Tonight, Sounds and Settings screens while no mix is loaded and, now and then, a full screen ad after you finish something, such as saving a mix or a check in. No ad appears while sound plays or fades, on the Player, in Lights Out or on the wake alarm, and every ad is muted. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. The app never passes your mixes, favourites, nights, check ins, alarm or feedback to the ads SDK, and ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG). We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you. How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads use your data, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this phone.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads (the exact path varies by phone). Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show non-personalized ads.

7. Feedback and email (optional)

Sending feedback is up to you. Only when you tap Send in Settings, Send feedback, the app sends the kind of feedback you chose, the message you typed, the time it was sent and the diagnostics shown on that screen before you send (phone model, Android version and LunaLull version) to Formspree (formspree.io), a form service that delivers it to us by email. Formspree, like any web service, also receives your IP address. Nothing is sent if you do not tap Send. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.

8. Notifications, alarms and the playback notification

Reminders, the wind down reminder, the wake alarm and its backup notification are scheduled on your phone with Android's alarm and notification services. While a mix plays or is paused, LunaLull keeps a media notification with its controls, which lets the sound continue with the screen off (an Android foreground service for media playback); it goes away when you stop the mix. LunaLull has no push service and no server that sends notifications, and it never registers for push messages.

9. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include LunaLull's data on this phone (your mixes, favourites, nights calendar, check ins, onboarding answers, settings and your ad choice) in that backup, and copy it when you move to a new phone. Downloaded sound files are left out of both. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings.

10. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on, the wake alarm backup and the playback controls.
  • Alarms and reminders (optional, you grant it in Android settings): so the wake alarm backup can ring at the exact minute. Without it the backup may be a few minutes late.
  • Media playback in the foreground: so sound keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Wake lock: so the wake alarm can start its sound on time.
  • Vibration: for haptic clicks.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, the optional downloads and feedback.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

LunaLull does not ask for the microphone, the camera, location, contacts or access to your files. You can deny or revoke any permission in Android settings and the rest of the app keeps working.

11. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information Google collects as described in section 5, the IP address our file hosting receives for a download, and the feedback you choose to send, the app shares nothing with anyone.

12. Purchases

LunaLull for Android has no in-app purchases. Every sound and feature is included at no cost, so the app never handles payment information.

13. Children

LunaLull is meant for adults, including parents and carers who play sounds for a baby. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child sent us feedback, email us and we will delete it.

14. Keeping and deleting information

Your data lives on your phone. To erase it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app; copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them there. We keep feedback messages only as long as we need them to answer you and improve the app, and we delete them when you ask. Information Google holds for advertising is governed by Google's policies and by your choices in Privacy choices and in your Android settings.

15. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, and to object to how it is used. LunaLull has no account, so we hold no profile about you. To make a request, email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

16. Security

Your information stays on your phone, in storage that only the app can read. Every connection the app makes uses encrypted HTTPS; the app refuses unencrypted connections.

17. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy, its effective date and the copy in the app.

18. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

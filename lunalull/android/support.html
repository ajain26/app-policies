LunaLull for Android: Help and Support

LunaLull for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps.

Email

Email us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com. A person reads every message. If something is not working, include your phone model and Android version.

In the app

In the app, Settings, Help answers the most common questions, and Settings, Send feedback attaches your device details for you and shows them before you send.

If sound stops overnight

If sound stops overnight, check the sleep timer first. If there is no timer, open LunaLull's app info in Android Settings, tap Battery and choose Unrestricted. Some phone makers add their own battery saver; allow LunaLull there too.

If reminders or the wake alarm never arrive

Turn on notifications for LunaLull in Android Settings. For the wake alarm backup to ring at the exact minute, also allow Alarms and reminders. Keep your phone's own alarm on if you have to be up at a fixed time.

Privacy and ads

The Privacy Policy explains what the app handles and where it goes. Ad choices are in Settings, Privacy choices, where the law requires them. To ask about or delete feedback you sent, email us.

Sound credits

Every field recording in the app is credited in the Terms of Use, section 13, and in each sound's details.

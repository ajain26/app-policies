How is the BiteScore calculated?

It combines the barometric pressure trend, wind, cloud cover, the water temperature (estimated from the air temperature), precipitation and the solunar major and minor feeding periods, weighted for the species you chose. The What's Driving It row on the Today screen shows the factors pushing your score up or down.

Where does the weather come from?

From MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute. Sunrise, sunset, the moon phase and the solunar periods are calculated on your phone.

Does the Coach use the internet?

No. The Coach is an automated, rule-based coach that runs entirely on your phone. Your questions never leave your device.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only when you tap My location on the Spots map, to show where you are. Your position stays on your phone. You choose your home water by searching for its name, and you can use the whole app without sharing your location.

When do bite alerts arrive?

Turn on the Daily Bite Alert in Settings to get one alert around 6 AM on EPIC and GOOD days. Android may deliver it a little later to save battery. If alerts stop, check that notifications for BiteWindow and its Bite alerts channel are allowed in Android settings.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, choose Widgets, find BiteWindow and drag Today's Bite into place. It shows your last forecast; open the app to refresh it.

Why do I see ads?

BiteWindow is free on Android and every feature is included. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it, and there is nothing to buy: a banner above the tab bar on the Today, Log and Settings screens and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to a tab after finishing something. Where privacy laws require it, you can change your ad choices in Settings, Privacy choices.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your phone. BiteWindow has no accounts and no servers, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes your data. If Android backup is on, Android can restore your profile, spots and catch log on a new phone.

Is BiteWindow a substitute for fishing regulations?

No. BiteWindow is informational only. Always follow your local fishing regulations, seasons, and limits.

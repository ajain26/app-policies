Privacy Policy

BiteWindow for Android. Effective date: October 31, 2026

BiteWindow for Android ("the app") is a fishing forecast app published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your profile, spots, catch log and photos stay on your phone, and we run no servers and have no accounts. To build forecasts, show maps and search for places, the app contacts the third-party services listed below, which receive the locations or search text involved and your IP address. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

BiteWindow for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your phone

You may enter the species you fish for, your water type, skill level, trips per month, biggest frustration, goal and target size, your home water and saved spots, and a catch log (dates, species, weights, baits, photos and the forecast attached to each catch). All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us: we operate no servers and cannot see, access or recover it.

  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not run analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

3. Weather forecasts (MET Norway)

To score your bite, the app requests weather forecasts from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) at api.met.no. Each request carries the coordinates of your home water, rounded to at most 4 decimal places (about 11 meters). MET Norway also receives your IP address and stores it in its logs. Requests identify the app and our contact email, not you. Forecasts are cached on your phone, and the app only asks for new data while you are using it. MET Norway's terms: https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService

4. Maps (OpenFreeMap)

The Spots map and the home water preview load map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which is delivered through Cloudflare. Tile requests reveal the map area you are viewing and include your IP address.

5. Place search

When you search for a water by name, the text you type is sent to your phone's built-in place search, which on most Android phones is provided by Google, to find matching places. In the Spots search the request also carries an area around your home water, so nearby places come first. If that search is not available on your phone, the app sends the same text and area to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) instead, which also receives your IP address.

6. Location

BiteWindow asks for location access only when you tap My location on the Spots map, and only while you are using the app. Approximate location is enough. Your position is used on your phone to show where you are on the map and is never sent to us or to any other service; the map then loads the tiles for that area from OpenFreeMap as described above. The app never uses your location in the background. You can use the whole app without location access: you choose your home water by searching for its name.

7. The Coach runs on your phone

The Coach is an automated, rule-based fishing coach built into the app. Your questions and its answers are processed entirely on your phone and are never sent to us or to any other service. The conversation is not saved after the app closes.

8. Bite alerts and the home screen widget

If you turn on the Daily Bite Alert, the app schedules a local notification on your phone for around 6 AM on EPIC and GOOD days, built from the last forecast, and schedules it again after the phone restarts or its clock or time zone changes. The Today's Bite widget shows the last forecast on your home screen. Neither fetches anything by itself, and no notification server is involved.

9. Advertising (Google AdMob)

BiteWindow is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Today, Log and Settings screens and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to a tab after finishing something, such as logging a catch. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. The app never passes your location, profile, spots, catch log, photos or Coach conversations to the ads SDK, and ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

10. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Without consent or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show non-personalized ads.

11. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include your profile, spots, catch log and settings in that backup and restore them on a new phone. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the phone can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. Catch photos are not part of the cloud backup; Android copies them only when you move directly to a new phone. Your ad choices and cached data are not included. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings.

12. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (approximate or precise): only for the My location button on the Spots map, only while you use the app.
  • Notifications: only for the Daily Bite Alert on EPIC and GOOD mornings.
  • Run at startup: to schedule the Daily Bite Alert again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: to load forecasts, maps, place search results and ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

Catch photos are added through Android's photo picker, so the app never asks for access to your photo library and only receives the photos you pick. Each permission is optional: you can deny or revoke it in Android settings and the rest of the app keeps working.

13. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described in section 9, and the locations, search text and IP address the services in sections 3 to 5 receive to do their job, the app shares nothing with anyone.

14. Purchases

BiteWindow has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free, so the app never handles payment information.

15. Children

BiteWindow is a general audience outdoors app for anglers aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

16. Deleting your data

Your data lives on your phone. To erase everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app; Android then removes the app's database, settings and catch photos. Deleting a catch in the app also deletes its photo. Copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them there. There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing was ever sent to us.

17. Security

Your information stays on your phone, in storage that only the app can read, protected by the phone's own security. Every connection the app makes uses encrypted HTTPS; the app refuses unencrypted connections.

18. Not professional advice

BiteWindow is informational only. Forecasts and Coach suggestions can be wrong. Always follow your local fishing regulations, seasons, and limits; the app never determines what is legal to catch or keep.

19. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change ships. The latest version is always available in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

20. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Current iOS release

SetSaga is a fitness planner and training journal for adults. The app works without an online account. Your training information is processed on your device; we do not receive your profile, measurements, workout history or companion questions from the app.

Information you choose to enter

The app stores the profile and preferences you provide, including your chosen name, age, training goals, experience, equipment, schedule, activity level, motivation and movement preferences. Height, weight and target weight are optional. Your journal can include completed workouts, logged sets, effort ratings, measurements, quests, rewards and custom routines.

This information is used on your device to create your plan, show your history and calculate progress. The offline companion processes your questions using local rules. It does not send them to an AI provider or other service.

Local storage and security

Your working profile and training journal in the iOS app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is held in the device’s secure Keychain. Security depends on your device, its passcode and the way you manage backups. No storage method can guarantee recovery or absolute security.

The current release upgrades readable older iPhone keys to allow migration through supported encrypted system backups. Open the updated app on the original phone before a future transfer. This does not enable iCloud Keychain synchronization or guarantee that Quick Start, an iCloud restore or another device transfer will preserve access to your training data.

Before switching phones: choose My progress → Export my progress on the original phone. Keep the backup somewhere private, then use Import a training backup on the new phone. If the original encryption key is missing, the encrypted records cannot be recovered without a compatible exported backup.

Files you export or import

An exported backup is a readable, unencrypted JSON file containing your profile, measurements, training history and progress. It does not contain encryption keys, sign-in tokens or reminder identifiers, and it omits an unfinished workout.

The app uses iOS file and sharing controls when you choose to export or import. Temporary readable copies may remain in the app’s cache until removed by the operating system or app removal. Any destination or person you choose can receive the exported information. If you save or share a file through a third-party app or cloud provider, that provider’s policies apply. Keep these files private and delete copies you no longer need.

A valid import asks for confirmation before replacing the app’s current progress. We do not upload these files to a developer-controlled service.

Permissions and reminders

Notification permission is optional. If you turn on the reminder, iOS schedules a local notification for 6 pm. The app does not use a remote push service for these reminders. You can turn the reminder off in My progress, use Turn off daily reminders on the storage recovery screen, or disable this app’s notifications in iOS Settings.

Files are accessed through the system picker or share sheet when you choose those actions. The app does not request HealthKit, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

No advertising, analytics or connected accounts

This release includes no advertising trackers or analytics. It does not support live AI, online accounts, cloud backup, online community sharing or subscriptions. The labeled demo contains sample progress and sample guild information; it does not connect you with other people.

Your controls and retention

Your local progress stays on your device until you replace or reset it, or remove the app. To remove the working profile and training history, use the reset option in My progress. Resetting also turns off the app’s daily reminders. If a reset cannot be saved, the app reports the problem and keeps the previous records.

Exported files and any copies you saved or shared are separate from the app’s working records and must be deleted where they were stored. Secure Keychain items may remain after a reset or uninstall. A Keychain item alone is not your training journal.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Do not send your backup, body measurements or other sensitive information unless you specifically want to share it for troubleshooting. Email services process the messages under their own policies.

This website and Apple services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, forms or cookies. GitHub processes normal hosting requests, which can include technical information such as your IP address; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Apple manages App Store and TestFlight delivery, feedback and diagnostics under its own terms and privacy policies.

Adults and general fitness

SetSaga is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment.

Updates and questions

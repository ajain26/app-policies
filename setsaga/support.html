SetSaga support

Help with your training journal, local reminders and progress backups.

Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com. Include your app version and build number, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help; remove personal information before sending it. Please do not attach a progress backup or body measurements unless you choose to share them for troubleshooting.

Moving to a new phone

  1. On the original phone, open the current app and choose My progress → Export my progress.
  2. Save the readable JSON backup somewhere private that you can access on the new phone.
  3. On the new phone, choose Import a training backup, select the file, review its details and confirm the restore.

Backups omit unfinished workouts and reminder settings. Re-enable your reminder on the new phone if you want it. Do not rely only on Quick Start or an iCloud/system restore: transfer of the encryption key is not guaranteed.

“Your data needs a little care”

Keep the original phone and any backup files while trying to recover your history. Unlock the phone and choose Retry saved data. If the records remain unreadable after moving phones, export from the original phone and select Import a training backup on the recovery screen.

If the encryption key is gone, we cannot recreate it or decrypt the old records. An exported backup is needed. Start fresh on this device replaces local progress after confirmation; choose it only when you are ready to do that.

Turning reminders off

Open My progress and choose Turn off beside the daily 6 pm reminder. On the recovery screen, choose Turn off daily reminders. You can also disable notifications for the app in iOS Settings. Leaving the demo with Create my profile and resetting progress cancel the app’s daily reminders.

Entering measurements

Height and weight support decimal values. For example, enter 75.5 or 75,5 for weight, then select Log weight in the body journal. Profile measurements may be left blank. Age must be a whole number between 18 and 100.

Workout timing and rewards

Workout time counts while the session is open in the foreground. Saving, closing or putting the app in the background pauses active training time. Recovery and Conditioning sessions require their displayed duration. Strength sessions require all sets and at least one active minute.

XP and coins for completed workouts are awarded once per workout category per day. Extra completed sessions still appear in your journal. Training is self-reported; the app does not detect exercise.

Offline guidance and the demo

The companion uses local rules for general fitness guidance. The current release has no live AI, connected accounts, cloud backup, online community or subscription. The demo’s guilds and rankings are labeled sample data. Create your own profile to keep personal progress.

If a movement causes pain or unusual symptoms, stop and seek advice from a qualified professional. For severe or urgent symptoms, seek immediate medical help.

Deleting or restoring progress

Export first if you want a copy. The reset option in My progress removes the app’s working profile and training history after confirmation. Delete any exported files separately wherever you saved or shared them. You can later restore a compatible backup with Import a training backup.

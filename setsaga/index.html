SetSaga brings personal workout plans, daily quests and a private training journal together on your iPhone or iPad.

Get supportRead our privacy policy

A plan that fits

Choose your goals, equipment and schedule. Follow strength, recovery and conditioning sessions, swap movements and build your own routines.

Progress you can see

Log sets, review personal records and optional body measurements, complete daily quests and earn XP, ranks and cosmetic rewards.

Your journal stays local

Use the app without an account. Your working training records are encrypted on your device. Export a private backup before switching phones.

Guidance that works offline

Ask the rule-based companion for general fitness guidance and set an optional local reminder for 6 pm.

Before switching phones, make a backup. Export from My progress on your original phone, keep the readable file private and import it on the new device. Device transfers do not guarantee access to encrypted records.

For adults aged 18 and over. Workouts and progress are self-reported. The app supports general fitness and does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. This release has no live AI, cloud backup, online community or subscription.

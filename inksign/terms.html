What InkSign does

InkSign lets you import or scan a document, place a signature, initials, text, or a date on it, and export or share the result. It also shows a plain language briefing of details it finds in a document, such as dates and amounts.

The summary is not legal advice

InkSign can point out details in a document, including terms that are often worth a second look. This is information, not legal advice. It is generated automatically, it may be incomplete, and it is never a substitute for reading the document itself or for advice from a qualified professional.

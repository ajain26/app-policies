InkSign for Android

Support

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and you will get a reply within two business days.

1. How do I get a document into InkSign?

Tap Sign a Document or Files to pick a PDF, Photos to turn up to 20 pictures into one document, or Scan to photograph a paper document with Google's document scanner. You can also share a PDF or an image to InkSign from another app, or choose InkSign under "Open with" when you open a PDF.

2. The document is asking for a password

Some PDFs are encrypted by whoever created them. InkSign asks for the password once and opens the document on your device. If you do not have the password, ask the sender for a copy without one.

InkSign keeps its own copy without the password and never stores the password itself.

3. Where do my signed documents go?

They stay in InkSign on your device until you export, share, save or print them. Open the document, tap the share button and prepare the signed PDF, then share it by mail, message or into any app that accepts files, save it to your device, or print it.

4. Is my document sent anywhere for analysis?

No. Everything happens on your device. Before You Sign reads the text on your device, using Google ML Kit when a page is a scan, and your document is never uploaded. Ads come from Google AdMob and never see your documents. See the Privacy Policy for details.

5. Is InkSign free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with ads from Google AdMob, and there are no purchases. Where the law requires it, Privacy choices in Settings changes your ad consent.

6. Where do ads appear?

InkSign shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the document list (once it lists a document) and of Settings, and now and then a full screen ad when you are back on the document list after you share, save or print a file or save a template. Ads never appear inside your documents or in the files you export, share, save or print, and never on the screens where you sign, manage pages, read Before You Sign, export or verify a document.

7. I have a new device. Where are my documents?

InkSign's documents are not part of Android's cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, your documents move to a new device only through Android's device-to-device transfer; on earlier versions they stay on this device. To keep a copy anywhere else, save each signed PDF to your device or share it to a place you choose.

8. Can I lock InkSign?

InkSign has no lock of its own. Use your device's screen lock, and an app lock or private space where your device offers one. On Android 13 and later, the recent apps screen does not show InkSign's pages.

9. Reporting a bug

Email us with your device model, your Android version, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not attach the document itself if it is confidential. A description of the problem is enough.

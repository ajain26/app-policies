InkSign for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

InkSign for Android ("InkSign" or "the app") is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). The short version: your documents, signatures and details stay on your device until you share, save or print a file yourself. InkSign has no account and no server of ours. The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads. This policy explains the details.

1. Who we are

InkSign for Android is an app for signing documents on your device. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Every document you import, scan or share to InkSign, the page images it is made from, every signature and set of initials you draw, type or photograph, the details you save for autofill such as your name or address, your templates and your settings are stored only in InkSign's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read that storage.

InkSign has no account and we run no server, so we cannot see, access or recover any of it.

3. Scanning, photos and files

Scan uses Google's document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services. InkSign holds no camera permission: the scanner takes the pictures and gives InkSign only the finished page images, which InkSign turns into a PDF on your device. Google Play services downloads the scanner the first time you use it.

Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker and PDFs with Android's file picker, so InkSign sees only the photos and files you choose. Items you share to InkSign from another app are copied into its private storage and read only there.

4. Reading the text of a document

Before You Sign reads the text of a document on your device to list the dates, amounts and terms it finds. When a page has no text of its own, as with a scan, InkSign recognizes the text in the page image with Google ML Kit's text recognition, which also runs on your device. Your documents, their page images, your signatures, your details and the recognized text are never sent to us or to Google.

To run and improve ML Kit and the document scanner, Google may collect diagnostic and usage data: device information (such as the manufacturer, the model and the Android version), app information (the package name and the app version), a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance metrics, settings such as the image format and size, input and output sizes, event types and error codes. Google sends this over an encrypted connection and says ML Kit does not transfer it to third parties (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

5. Ads

InkSign shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the document list (once it lists a document) and of Settings, and now and then a full screen ad when you are back on the document list after you share, save or print a file or save a template. Ads never appear inside your documents or in the files you export, share, save or print, and never on the screens where you sign, manage pages, read Before You Sign, export or verify a document. The ads are limited to content Google rates as suitable for most audiences with parental guidance (PG).

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google: your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device; your interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views; diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use; and device identifiers: the advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on your device. Google encrypts this data in transit.

Ad requests from InkSign never contain your documents, your signatures, your details or anything you type. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choice about ads, and InkSign starts the ads only once that tool says ads may be requested. The tool keeps your choice on your device and contacts Google to find out which message applies to you.

Privacy choices in Settings lets you review or change your choice where it applies. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

7. Permissions

InkSign uses Android's internet and network state permissions for the ads, Google's consent tools and ML Kit's diagnostics, and the advertising ID permission for the ads. It asks for no camera, storage, photo, contacts, location, microphone or notification permission.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not offer or require accounts, logins or registration.
  • We do not send your documents, signatures or details to a server of ours. There is none.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Apart from what Google collects for ads and diagnostics, as described above, nothing about you leaves your device unless you send a file somewhere yourself.

9. Sharing, saving and printing

Files leave your device only when you choose to share, save or print them. Where they go then is up to you and the app, service or printer you choose.

10. The signing certificate

If you attach a signing certificate when you export, the exported file gets an extra page and a fingerprint record with the signing and export times, the type of device (an Android phone or tablet), the Android version, the app version and, if you saved one in Your Details, your name. That record travels with the file you send, so anyone who receives the file can read it.

11. Android backup and device transfer

InkSign's documents, signatures, templates, details and settings are not included in Android's cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, your documents move to a new device only through Android's device-to-device transfer; on earlier versions they stay on this device.

What the ad and consent tools keep on your device, such as the ad WebView's data and your ad choice, is never part of a backup or a device transfer.

12. Security

Your documents are kept in InkSign's private storage, which Android keeps from other apps, and Android encrypts it on devices with encryption turned on. Use a screen lock to keep others out of your device. No storage is perfectly secure, so keep your own copy of anything important.

13. Deleting your data

Deleting a document in InkSign removes it from your device. Uninstalling InkSign deletes everything it stored, unless you choose to keep its data when Android offers to. Because we hold none of your documents, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

For the information Google collects to show ads, use Privacy choices in Settings, the advertising ID settings on your device and the tools Google provides.

14. Children

InkSign is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Changes to this policy

If a new version of InkSign changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version ships, and describe plainly what changed.

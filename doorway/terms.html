What Doorway does

Doorway lets you set limits on apps you choose. When a limit is reached, iOS blocks the app. You can spend one of three daily doors to get five more minutes of use. Doorway works through Apple's Screen Time frameworks and depends on them working as Apple intends.

Free and Pro

The free version lets you limit one app with a daily time limit and doors. Doorway Pro adds limits for any number of apps, schedules and Beast Mode.

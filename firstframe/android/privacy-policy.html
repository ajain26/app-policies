Privacy Policy

Firstframe for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Firstframe for Android ("the app") is a baby milestone journal for parents, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the things you record about your child and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your child's name, dates, photos, notes and milestones stay on this device and are never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Firstframe for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What you record stays on this device

Everything you enter is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: each child's name or nickname, birth date or due date, the areas you chose to focus on, the milestones you log with their dates and notes, the photos you attach, the activities you mark done, and your onboarding answers.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • Ads never receive any of it: no name, date, photo, note or milestone is passed to the ad service.

3. Photos

When you add a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app only the one photo you choose. The app does not ask for access to your photo library, storage or camera. It keeps its own resized copy of the photo inside the app; the original in your gallery is not changed. Removing a memory or a child, or uninstalling the app, deletes the app's copies.

4. Cards you share or save

A milestone card is an image the app makes on this device. It leaves the app only when you share it or save it, and it goes only where you choose, such as a messaging app or a folder. Once a card leaves the app, the app or service you sent it to decides how it is handled.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Firstframe is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Timeline, Milestones, Today and Settings screens and, now and then, a full screen ad after you finish logging a first or making a card. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads in the app are not personalized on the basis of anything you record about your child, and they are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

7. Window heads-ups

If you allow notifications, the app shows a short note a few days before a new development window opens for your child. These notes are planned and shown by this device; nothing about them is sent to us.

8. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include the app's database, settings and photo copies in that backup and restore them to a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices are not included.

9. Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on window heads-ups, at the end of setup or in Settings. You can deny it, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Internet and network state: for ads and Google's privacy message.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Run at startup and keep awake: used by Android's scheduler so a planned heads-up still arrives after your phone restarts.

The app does not ask for access to your photo library, files, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

10. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

11. Children

Firstframe is made for parents and other adult caregivers aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children and is not meant to be used by them. The information you record about your child stays on this device as described in section 2; we do not collect it, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

12. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Remove a memory from its screen, or a child with all of their memories and photos in Settings; or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app stored on this device. Cards you shared or saved elsewhere, and copies in a Google backup, stay until you delete them.

13. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

15. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

Where are my photos and memories stored?

Only on this device, inside the app. The app keeps its own copy of each photo you attach, so the original stays in your gallery. To keep a memory somewhere else, open it, tap Make a card and share or save the card.

How do I log a first?

Tap Log a first on the Timeline, or tap a milestone on the Milestones tab. Pick the date it happened, add a photo and a note if you like, and save. Search finds every milestone, so earlier firsts can be added any time.

What are development windows?

Nine named month ranges from birth to age two, each with the milestones that often appear in it. They show when many babies get there, never a deadline: every baby's timeline is a range. Firstframe is educational, not medical advice. Talk to your pediatrician about your child's development.

I did not get a window heads-up

Check that notifications are allowed for Firstframe in your Android settings, or turn them on in Settings, Notifications. Heads-ups come a few days before a window opens, in the daytime, and Android can deliver them late to save battery.

How do I add a sibling or edit a birth date?

Open Settings. Tap a child to change the name, photo or dates, or tap Add a child. When a baby you were expecting arrives, open them and tap They've arrived to set the birthday.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Firstframe free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar, and now and then a full screen ad appears after you log a first or make a card. Ads never see your child's name, photos or milestones. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

1. What Firstframe is

Firstframe is a journal for parents: it shows typical developmental windows and milestones from birth to age two, lets you log your child's firsts with photos and notes, suggests simple play ideas, and makes milestone cards to share. Its milestone content is evidence-informed, based on public CDC milestone data, and written as general guidance.

Firstframe is educational. It is not a medical device, it does not screen, assess or diagnose anything, and it gives no medical advice. Every baby's timeline is a range. For any question about your child's health or development, talk to your pediatrician or another qualified professional.

2. Play ideas and safety

Play ideas are for an awake child with an adult right there and hands-on the whole time. Use your own judgment about what suits your child, skip anything that does not, never leave your child unattended, and follow safe sleep guidance from your pediatrician at sleep time.

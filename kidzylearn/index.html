Kidzy Learn

ABC, phonics and tracing for ages 2 to 7.

🔤 Nine learning worlds

Alphabet, phonics, numbers, tracing, spelling, colors, shapes, quizzes and puzzles, in three age levels for 2 to 3, 4 to 5, and 6 to 7.

🔒 Nothing about your child leaves the iPhone

No account, no server of ours, no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Apart from the App Store, the app makes no network requests, so every activity works offline.

🧮 A grown-up gate

Buying, restoring, sharing and the web links out of the app all sit behind a math question meant for an adult.

🏅 Four children, one app

Each child keeps their own progress, streak and badges. Alphabet and Numbers are free forever, with no trial and no purchase.

Kidzy Learn is an educational play app for children roughly ages 2 to 7. It is play rather than assessment: streaks, badges and scores exist to bring a child back tomorrow, and they measure nothing about how a child is doing. Premium is a single one-time purchase, never a subscription.

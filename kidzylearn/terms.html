What Kidzy Learn is

Kidzy Learn is a learning and play app for children roughly ages 2 to 7. It has nine activities: the alphabet, phonics, numbers, tracing, spelling, colors, shapes, quizzes and puzzles, with three age levels of 2 to 3, 4 to 5, and 6 to 7. The activities work offline, it contains no advertising, and it has no chat, no camera, no other users and no way for a child to reach anyone.

It is play, not assessment

Kidzy Learn is an educational play app. It is not a test, an assessment, a screening tool or a diagnosis. Streaks, badges, scores and the learning journey exist to encourage a child to come back tomorrow. They do not measure ability, they are not compared against any standard or against any other child, and nothing in the app should be read as a statement that a child is ahead or behind. If you have any concern about your child's speech, hearing, vision, learning or development, speak to your pediatrician, your health visitor or your child's teacher.

The grown-up gate

Anything that spends money, and every link that opens a web page, is placed behind a math question that a young child is not expected to answer: buying, restoring a purchase, starting the free trial, opening the unlock screen, opening the support, privacy or terms links at the foot of it, sharing a progress card, and renaming or deleting a child's profile. Three wrong answers pause the gate briefly before it can be tried again.

Some things sit outside the gate on purpose, because they spend nothing and reveal nothing: switching between children, printing a tracing worksheet, the reminder explainer that can appear on the home screen once a streak has started, and the shortcut that opens the iOS Settings app if notifications were declined.

The gate is a sensible barrier, not a security system, and an older or determined child may work out the answer. It does not replace your own device settings. If you want a hard stop, turn off in-app purchases in Apple's Screen Time settings.

What Kidzy Learn is

Kidzy Learn is a learning and play app for children roughly ages 2 to 7. It has nine activities: the alphabet, phonics, numbers, tracing, spelling, colors, shapes, quizzes and puzzles, with three age levels of 2 to 3, 4 to 5, and 6 to 7. The activities work offline. It has no chat, no camera, no other users and no way for a child to message anyone.

The grown-up gate

These are placed behind a math question that a young child is not expected to answer: opening the support, privacy or terms pages, sharing a progress card, printing a tracing worksheet, turning the daily reminder on, and renaming or deleting a child's profile. Three wrong answers pause the gate briefly before it can be tried again.

Some things sit outside the gate on purpose, because they reveal nothing: switching between children, choosing "Maybe Later" on the reminder explainer, and turning the reminder off. Ads are outside it too, because they are shown by Google's code rather than ours (see Free, with ads, above).

The gate is a sensible barrier, not a security system, and an older or determined child may work out the answer. It does not replace your own device settings, such as Google Family Link.

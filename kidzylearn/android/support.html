Kidzy Learn for Android

Support

Questions, bugs and ads

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and a real person will reply, usually within a couple of days. There is no form to fill in and no ticket number to keep.

This is the support page for the Android version of Kidzy Learn, listed on Google Play as "Kidzy: ABC Phonics & Tracing" and published by Anshul Apps. It helps to say which phone or tablet and which Android version you are using, and which activity the problem happens in. Nothing about your child is needed to help you. Kidzy Learn runs on Android 8.0 and newer.

Common questions

Is Kidzy Learn free?

Yes. Every activity is open from the first launch, and this version has nothing to buy: no in-app purchases and no subscription. Kidzy Learn is supported by ads from Google AdMob, set up for children.

What kind of ads does it show, and where?

A banner at the bottom of the home screen, the screen where your child picks an activity, in a space kept free for it so nothing moves when an ad loads. Now and then a full-screen ad, only when your child leaves an activity after finishing a round, before the home screen comes back. Ads never interrupt an activity, and a full-screen ad never appears when the app opens, within three minutes of it opening, or over the home screen. They are text and image ads, some of them animated or interactive, and never video, and a full-screen ad has its own close button.

To close a full-screen ad, wait for the short countdown that Google's ad code runs, within about 5 seconds, then tap the ad's close button. The Back button may not close it.

Every ad request is marked as child-directed, so Google turns off personalized ads and remarketing, and ad content is limited to general audiences (G). The app has no permission to read Android's advertising ID. The Privacy Policy lists the technical information Google's ad code sends to Google.

My child tapped an ad

Tapping an ad can open the advertiser's web page or Google Play outside Kidzy. Press Back, or switch back to Kidzy Learn, to return. Ads are the one thing the grown-up gate cannot cover, because Google's code shows them. If you would like a parent's approval before anything is installed from Google Play, Google Family Link can require it.

I saw an ad that should not be in a kids app

Please email us what the ad showed and roughly when you saw it, with a screenshot if you have one. Google chooses each ad within the settings we set, and we can block a specific advertiser in AdMob once we know about it.

Can I turn the ads off?

Not in this version. The ads are what keep every activity free. With no internet connection no new ads can load, and every activity still works.

What is the math question for?

It is the grown-up gate. It stands in front of the support, privacy and terms pages (at the foot of the progress screen), sharing a progress card, printing a tracing worksheet, turning the daily reminder on, and renaming or deleting a child's profile.

A few things sit outside it on purpose, because they reveal nothing: switching between children, choosing "Maybe Later" on the reminder explainer, and turning the reminder off. The gate is a sensible barrier rather than a security system, so pair it with Google Family Link if you have an older child who enjoys a challenge.

The gate stopped accepting answers

After three wrong answers in a row it pauses for about half a minute, then lets you try again with a new question. That pause is deliberate, and closing and reopening the app does not skip it.

Can I set up more than one child?

Yes, up to four. Each child keeps their own progress, streak and badges, and switching between them takes a tap on their avatar, with no gate, so a child can pick their own animal. Renaming or deleting a profile does need the gate.

Changing a name, or not giving one at all

The last screen of the introduction asks for a first name, and the "Let's Go" button stays dimmed and does nothing until there is something in the box. Adding a second, third or fourth child asks for a name too. A nickname or a first initial works perfectly well, and is what we would suggest.

To change a name later, open the profile switcher and choose rename, which asks the grown-up gate first. The rename box will not save a blank name: the green tick stays greyed out until you type something, so a name can be replaced rather than cleared.

If you would rather no name were saved at all, use the Skip button in the top corner of the introduction, on any screen before the last one. That finishes setup without asking for a name. The home screen then greets your child with a wave, and the weekly progress card says "your little learner".

Which age level should I choose?

There are three: 2 to 3, 4 to 5, and 6 to 7. Choose the one that matches how your child plays today rather than their exact age, and move them to a different level if the activities feel too easy or too hard. The level belongs to each child's profile, so siblings are unaffected.

Why does my child's name turn up in the spelling game?

On purpose, and children love it. The name joins the list of words to spell when it is exactly three, four or five letters long, matching the word lengths of the Easy, Medium and Hard rounds. Names of any other length are left out. Most words have a recording built into the app; anything without one, including a name, is read out by the text-to-speech engine that is part of Android on your device (usually Speech Services by Google). Kidzy Learn sends nothing anywhere to do it, and the name is never given to the ads. If you would rather it did not appear, rename the profile (behind the grown-up gate) to a nickname of a different length. A blank name cannot be saved.

Turning the daily reminder off, or on

There is a Daily reminder switch on the progress screen. Turning it on asks the grown-up gate first, and on Android 13 and newer Android then asks whether Kidzy Learn may send notifications; turning it off is a single tap and cancels the reminder straight away.

A short explainer can also appear on the home screen once a streak has started, offering "Turn On Reminders" or "Maybe Later". On Android, "Turn On Reminders" asks the grown-up gate first too, so a child cannot use up Android's notification prompt.

There is only ever one reminder, shown by your own device once a day around 5 in the afternoon (Android may deliver it a little later to save battery), with wording that never changes. If notifications were declined, the app offers a button that opens Kidzy Learn's notification settings. You can also find them in Android Settings, under Apps, Kidzy Learn, Notifications, where the reminder is listed as "Daily learning reminder".

The reminder stopped arriving

Android deletes an app's scheduled reminders when the app is force stopped, and some battery savers do the same. Kidzy Learn schedules the reminder again the next time it is opened, as long as notifications are still allowed. If they were switched off in Android Settings, the switch on the progress screen shows off, and turning it back on (after the gate) asks again.

There is no sound

Kidzy Learn plays through the media volume, which is separate from the ringer on most phones, so press the volume up button while the app is open on screen. If headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are connected, the sound is going there instead. All the audio is built into the app, so it works with no connection.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every activity works offline, including in airplane mode. Only the ads need a connection. Without one, Kidzy Learn cannot load new ads, but an ad it loaded earlier, while the device was online, can still appear: the banner already showing on the home screen, or a full-screen ad when your child leaves an activity. The app has no server of its own to talk to.

Is anything collected about my child?

Kidzy Learn itself collects nothing: there is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the name, avatar, age level and progress stay on your device. The one thing that sends data is Google's ad code, which sends Google technical information such as the device's IP address, basic device and app details, ad views and taps, and performance diagnostics, for requests that are marked as child-directed and carry no advertising ID. The Privacy Policy sets this out in detail.

Where is progress stored, and what happens if I uninstall the app?

On the device. If Android backup to your Google account is switched on, or you copy apps to a new phone when setting it up, Android may also bring Kidzy Learn's progress across with it. Uninstalling Kidzy Learn removes every profile, streak, badge and score from the device, and there is no copy on a server of ours to restore, because there is no server.

Deleting your data

A grown-up can delete a child's profile from the profile switcher, after the gate, as long as another profile remains. Clearing Kidzy Learn's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything Kidzy Learn stored on the device. Any copy in your Android backup is managed in your Google account. There is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to erase, since we never received any of it.

Can I print a tracing worksheet?

Yes, from the progress screen, after the grown-up gate. It opens the normal Android print screen, where you can send it to a printer or save it as a PDF. The worksheet is a plain practice page: no name, no avatar and no scores on it, so it is safe to hand around a classroom. The six letters are picked from the ones your child has not opened yet, but the page itself says nothing about that.

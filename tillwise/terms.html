What Tillwise does

Tillwise plans a week of dinners from its own recipes to fit the household size, weekly dinner budget and diets you choose, and builds one shopping list grouped by aisle. It prices that list from bundled average prices and, once you scan or import your grocery receipts, from the prices you actually paid.

Prices and savings are estimates

Average prices come from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Average Price Data, U.S. city average, as of the month shown in the app. Items the Bureau does not publish use Tillwise estimates and are marked "Est." in the app. Prices at your store will differ, and they change over time. Receipt text is read automatically and can be misread, which is why you check every line before it is saved. Savings compared with takeout and meal kits are estimates based on the per serving amounts printed in the app. Plan totals are a guide, not a quote.

Food, diets and allergies

Recipes are general home cooking ideas, not medical or nutrition advice. Diet labels such as Vegetarian, Gluten-free and Dairy-free mean that none of the listed ingredients is flagged for that diet in Tillwise's ingredient data. Brands and products vary, so always check ingredient labels, especially if you avoid an ingredient for health reasons or have a food allergy. Protein amounts are estimates. Follow safe food handling and cook meat, poultry, fish and eggs thoroughly.

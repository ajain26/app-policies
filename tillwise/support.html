Common questions

How is my plan priced?

Every item on your shopping list is priced as whole packages, because you buy a whole bag of rice, not a cup of it. Prices start from U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics city averages, with Tillwise estimates for items the Bureau does not publish. Each item shows its source: "Yours" for a price from your receipts, "US avg" for a Bureau average and "Est." for a Tillwise estimate. The Settings screen has a "Where prices come from" page with the details.

How do I use my own prices?

Open the Prices tab and tap "Scan a receipt", or "Import a photo" to use a picture you already took. Tillwise reads the receipt on your device, matches each line to an ingredient, and shows you every line to check before saving. Saved prices replace the averages in every plan straight away.

A receipt line matched the wrong ingredient

On the "Check your receipt" screen, tap the ingredient under the line and pick the right one, or choose "Skip this line". Tillwise remembers your correction, so the same line matches correctly next time. To remove a price you already saved, swipe it away in "Your price book".

The receipt could not be read

Lay the receipt flat in good light and fit the whole receipt in the frame. Long receipts can be scanned as several pages. Faded thermal paper is hard for any scanner to read; a clear photo taken soon after shopping works best.

I said no to camera access

You can turn it on any time in the Settings app under Tillwise, or keep using "Import a photo", which needs no permission.

How do I change my budget, household or diets?

Open the Settings tab in Tillwise and change the Household section. This week's plan is rebuilt to match, and any nights you locked stay.

I restored my subscription and Pro is still locked

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that bought the subscription, then open Tillwise, go to Settings and tap "Restore purchases". If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we will help.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tillwise, then Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in Tillwise's Settings. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Are my receipts sent anywhere?

No. Receipts are read on your device, the images are not stored, and Tillwise contains no networking code at all. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In Tillwise, open Settings. "Clear my prices" removes your price book and receipts. "Delete all my data" removes everything and starts the app fresh.

Reporting a bug

Email us with your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. If a receipt was misread, a description of the line is enough; please cover any card or personal details before sending a photo.

Tillwise

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

The short version: your plans, receipts and prices stay on your device. Tillwise has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers of ours. The app contains no networking code at all.

Who we are

Tillwise is an iOS app that plans a week of budget dinners and one shopping list, priced from average grocery prices and from your own receipts. It is developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us").

What stays on your device

Tillwise stores the following only in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Your household answers: how many people you feed, your weekly dinner budget, how many dinners to plan, any diets and your goals.
  • Your weekly dinner plans, which nights you locked, and which list items you checked off.
  • Your price book: for each receipt line you choose to keep, the cleaned item text, the price, the package size, the store name printed on the receipt and the date.
  • A short record of each receipt you saved: store name, date, subtotal, total and how many prices you kept.
  • Corrections you make when matching a receipt line to an ingredient, so the same line matches next time.
  • Which pantry staples you have on hand.

We operate no servers and have no ability to see, access, or recover any of it. Because the data lives on your device, it is protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption, and it is included in your device backups if you have those enabled. Backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

The camera and your receipts

If you allow camera access, Tillwise uses it for one purpose: scanning a grocery receipt when you tap "Scan a receipt". iOS asks for permission the first time you do this. If you decline, every other part of the app still works, and you can import a photo of a receipt instead.

Receipt text is read entirely on your device using Apple's built in text recognition. The receipt image is never uploaded and is never stored: once the text is read, the image is discarded. You review every line before anything is saved, and only the lines you keep are saved. Card numbers, payment details, addresses, phone numbers and approval codes printed on a receipt are skipped and never saved.

Importing a photo

"Import a photo" opens the iOS photo picker. Tillwise does not ask for access to your photo library and cannot browse it. Only the single photo you pick is passed to the app, its text is read on your device, and the photo itself is not stored by Tillwise.

Where average prices come from

The average prices Tillwise starts with are bundled inside the app. They come from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Average Price Data, plus Tillwise estimates for items the Bureau does not publish. The app does not download prices and does not look up your location or your stores.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your data. The app has no networking code.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign in.
  • There are no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no third party data sharing.
  • We do not sell or share any information about you, because we never receive any.

Purchases

Tillwise Pro is sold as an auto renewing subscription through Apple. Payment, billing, and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. We receive no personal information from Apple about who purchased.

Children

Tillwise is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings has "Clear my prices", which removes your price book and receipts, and "Delete all my data", which removes everything Tillwise stored. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

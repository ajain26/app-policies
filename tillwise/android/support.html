Where is my data stored?

Entirely on this device: your household, plans, pantry and price book. There is no account.

What do Yours, US avg and Est. mean?

Every price shows where it came from. Yours is a price from a receipt you saved. US avg is the U.S. city average from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, with data through August 2026. Est. is a Tillwise estimate for an item the Bureau does not track. Your receipt prices replace both in every plan.

How do I scan a receipt?

Open Your prices and tap Scan a receipt, or Import a photo. Lay the receipt flat in good light and capture all of it. Tillwise reads it on the phone, then shows Check your receipt, where you can fix the store, date, total, each price and each matched item before you save.

How do I change my household, budget or diets?

Settings, Household. Saving the changes rebuilds this week's plan; dinners you locked stay.

How do I start over?

Settings, Delete all my data removes everything and opens the setup again. Clear my prices removes only your price book and receipts.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Tillwise free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on This week, Your prices and Settings, and now and then a full screen ad appears at a natural break, such as after you save a receipt. Ads never appear on your shopping list, recipes or receipt screens. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

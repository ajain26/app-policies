Privacy Policy

Tillwise for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Tillwise for Android ("the app") plans a week of dinners on a grocery budget and prices it from your own receipts. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your household, plans, receipts and prices stay on this device and are never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Tillwise for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your information stays on this device

Everything you enter or save is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: your household size, weekly dinner budget, number of dinners, diets and goals, your weekly plans and the items you tick on the shopping list, your pantry staples, and your price book (for each receipt you keep: the store name, the date, the total and the cleaned item lines you chose to save, with their prices, plus the ingredient matches you corrected).

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The built-in recipes and the U.S. average price table ship inside the app; the app does not look up prices online.

3. Receipts (Google document scanner and Google ML Kit)

When you tap Scan a receipt, Google's document scanner opens. It is part of Google Play services, which may download it the first time, and it uses the camera inside Google Play services, so the app itself has no camera permission. Import a photo opens Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the one picture you choose.

The text of the receipt is then read on this device by Google ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app. The receipt image and its text are not sent to Google or to us. The image is used once to read the text and is not kept. Only the items you keep on the Check your receipt screen are saved, with the store name, date and total. Card numbers and addresses are never saved.

To keep these features working, Google ML Kit and the document scanner send Google diagnostic data over HTTPS: device information such as the model and Android version, the app's package name and version, a per installation identifier, performance metrics, the feature's settings and error codes. Google states that ML Kit does not transfer this data to third parties.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tillwise is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the This week, Your prices and Settings screens and, now and then, a full screen ad at a natural break, such as after you save a receipt. No ad is requested before you finish setting up your first week. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your plans, receipts or prices: none of them is passed to the ad service. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

6. Sharing your shopping list

When you share your shopping list, the text goes only where you choose with Android's share sheet, such as a messaging or notes app. Once it leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled.

7. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include the app's data (your household, plans, pantry and price book) in that backup and restore it to a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices are not included.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads, Google's privacy message, and the document scanner and ML Kit features of Google Play services.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library or files, and it sends no notifications.

9. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects and the diagnostics described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

10. Children

Tillwise is meant for people aged 13 and over who plan and buy groceries. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Clear my prices in Settings removes your price book and receipts. Delete all my data in Settings removes everything the app stored and starts over. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app does the same. Copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them there.

12. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

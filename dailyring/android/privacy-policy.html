Privacy Policy

Daily Ring for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Daily Ring for Android ("the app") is a habit tracker, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the habits you track and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your habits stay on this device and are never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Daily Ring for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your habits stay on this device

Everything you enter is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: your habits with their names, descriptions, icons, colors and categories, the days you checked them off, streaks, milestones and streak shields, reminder times, and your settings.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Daily Ring is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on Home, Reports and Settings and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to Home after adding a habit or to Settings after a backup. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your habit data: no habit name, check-off, streak or reminder is passed to the ad service. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

5. Reminders and the home screen widget

Habit reminders are scheduled and shown by this device, at the times you choose. The home screen widget reads your habits on this device and checks them off there. Nothing about either is sent to us.

6. Backup files you export

A backup you export from Settings is a file that holds your habits and their history. It goes only where you choose, such as a folder on your device, an email or a cloud drive. Once a file leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled. When you import a backup, the system file picker gives the app only the one file you choose.

7. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include your habits and settings in that backup and restore them to a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices are not included.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a habit reminder. You can deny it, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads and Google's privacy message.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not use exact alarms.

9. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

10. Children

Daily Ring is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Use Delete All Data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; each removes everything the app stored on this device. Backup files you saved elsewhere, and copies in a Google backup, stay until you delete them.

12. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

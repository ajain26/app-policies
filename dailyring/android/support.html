Where are my habits stored?

Only on this device. Use Settings, Export Data, to keep a copy.

How do I move my habits to a new phone?

Use Settings, Export Data, and copy the file to the new phone. Install Daily Ring there and open Settings, Import Data, then choose the file. Importing replaces the habits on the new phone.

How do streaks and streak shields work?

A streak counts the days in a row you checked a habit off. Today does not break it until the day is over. Every 14 days of streak, and each milestone from two weeks on, earns a streak shield; if you miss yesterday, a shield can fill it from the habit's details.

My reminder came late or did not come

Check that notifications are allowed for Daily Ring in your Android settings. To save battery, Android can deliver a reminder up to about an hour late. A reminder is skipped on a day you already checked that habit off.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, choose Widgets, find Daily Ring and drag it to your home screen. Larger sizes let you check habits off from the widget.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Daily Ring free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on Home, Reports and Settings, and now and then a full screen ad appears after you add a habit or finish a backup. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

Common questions

My reminders are not showing up.

Check that notifications are allowed for Daily Ring in the iOS Settings app, that the habit's Daily Reminder is turned on, and that a Focus mode is not silencing it.

Why does my streak still show before I check off today?

The day is not over yet, so an unfinished today does not break your streak. If the day ends without a check-off, the streak resets.

How do I move my habits to a new iPhone?

Use Settings > Export Data on your old iPhone, then Settings > Import Data on the new one. Habits also come along if you restore the new iPhone from a backup.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Delete All Data permanently removes all habits and history, or simply delete the app.

Does Daily Ring cost anything?

No. Daily Ring is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

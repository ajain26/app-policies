Privacy Policy & Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

The short version: your sleep data belongs to you and is stored on your iPhone. Audio is analyzed on your device and never uploaded. Rested has no accounts, no analytics SDKs and no ads. The only time anything about your sleep leaves your iPhone is when you ask the Sleep Coach a question, as explained under Sleep Coach answers.

Who we are

Rested is an iOS sleep tracking app developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us"). Because Rested works with health-related information (your sleep), this document serves as both our general privacy policy and our Health Data Privacy Policy as required by Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

Sleep data stored on your device

When you track a night, Rested records movement information from your iPhone's motion sensors, the times you went to bed and woke up, computed metrics such as your Sleep Score, and any notes, mood ratings, or manual entries you add. All of this is stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone. We have no ability to see, access, or recover the sleep data stored on your device.

Because the data lives on your device, it is protected by your device passcode and iOS encryption, and it is included in your device backups if you have iCloud or computer backups enabled. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Microphone and audio: on-device only

If you allow microphone access, Rested listens during your sleep session to detect snoring, coughing, and sleep talking. This analysis happens entirely on your device. Raw audio is never transmitted anywhere. Short clips of detected events (for example, a few seconds of snoring) are saved locally on your iPhone only so you can play them back in your morning report, and you can delete them at any time. If you deny microphone access, tracking still works using motion only.

Sleep Coach answers

When you ask the Sleep Coach a question, Rested sends your question, your recent messages in that conversation, and a summary of your sleep statistics over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to an AI service provider that writes the answer. The summary contains computed figures such as your latest Sleep Score, time asleep, bedtime and wake time, noise events, 7-night averages, streak, sleep goal and any tags you added, and it can include sleep imported from Apple Health. General questions (for example about naps or caffeine) are sent without your sleep statistics. Questions the coach answers on its own, such as greetings, app help and anything outside sleep, are not sent.

Audio, recordings, raw sensor data, your name and your contact details are never sent. Our server does not store your questions, your sleep summary or the answers. It keeps only what is needed to apply usage limits: a random install ID, kept in your iPhone's Keychain, with the number of free answers used, and for Rested Pro members the original transaction ID of the purchase with a daily count. The AI service provider processes the request to produce the answer, does not use it to train its models or for advertising, and may keep it for a limited period under its own data policy for safety and abuse monitoring.

This data is used only to answer your questions. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to track you.

HealthKit / Apple Health

With your permission, Rested reads sleep, heart rate, and heart-rate-variability data from Apple Health (for example, data recorded by an Apple Watch) to improve the accuracy of your sleep analysis, and writes the sleep sessions it records back to Apple Health so your data is in one place.

  • HealthKit data is used solely to provide the app's sleep analysis features.
  • We never use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we never sell it. This is both our policy and an Apple requirement.
  • Sleep imported from Apple Health becomes part of your nights in Rested, so the summary sent with a Sleep Coach question about your sleep can include it, as described under Sleep Coach answers. Otherwise HealthKit data stays within the app and Apple Health on your device.
  • You can revoke Health access at any time in the iOS Settings app or from Rested's own Settings → Permissions screen, and the app will continue to work without it.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts, logins, or registration.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent personal or health information, and we share it only as described under Sleep Coach answers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Rested Pro subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app checks your subscription status on-device using Apple's StoreKit framework. To give Rested Pro members unlimited Sleep Coach answers, the app sends Apple's signed record of your purchase to our server, which checks Apple's signature and keeps only the purchase's original transaction ID and a daily answer count. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Rested schedules local reminders on your device (bedtime wind-down, morning report ready, and a reminder before a free trial ends). These are generated on your iPhone; no notification data leaves your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

Settings → Data lets you export all of your sleep data as a file you own, and permanently delete all data the app has stored. Deleting the app from your device also removes its local database (subject to any device backups you keep).

Not a medical device

Rested provides sleep estimates for general wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your sleep or health, consult a qualified physician.

Children

Rested is intended for adults and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

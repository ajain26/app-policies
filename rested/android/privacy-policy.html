Privacy Policy

Rested: Sleep Tracker & Coach for Android. Effective date: October 23, 2026

Rested: Sleep Tracker & Coach for Android ("Rested", "the app") is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. Because sleep is health information, it is also Rested's health data privacy policy. In short: your nights, notes and sound clips stay on your phone, Rested has no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and the ad software and Google's sound recognition library send some technical information to Google. None of it includes your sleep data or audio.

1. Who we are

Rested for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your sleep data stays on your phone

When you track a night, Rested records movement readings from your phone's motion sensor, the times you went to bed and woke up, and the scores it calculates. It also keeps the moods, notes, factor tags and manual entries you add, your profile (sleep goal, target bedtime and wake time), your saved sound mixes and your settings. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

Rested does not connect to Health Connect or any other health service, and it does not read or write health data in other apps.

3. Microphone and sound clips

If you allow the microphone, Rested listens only during a night that you start, to detect snoring, coughing and sleep talking. The sound is analyzed on your phone by the YAMNet sound model running in Google's MediaPipe library, and audio is never uploaded. When Rested detects one of these sounds it saves a clip of about 4 seconds in its private storage, so you can play it in your Morning Report. A clip leaves your phone only if you tap Share clip and choose where to send it.

Clips are deleted when you discard or delete the night they belong to and when you delete all data. Clips older than 14 days are removed the next time Rested listens. Without the microphone, Rested tracks your nights with motion only.

4. Sound recognition statistics (Google MediaPipe)

MediaPipe, the Google library that runs the sound model on your phone, sends Google metrics about the performance and use of the sound recognizer: device information such as the phone model, Android version and available processors; the app's package name and version; the recognizer's settings; events such as when it starts and stops; error codes; performance measurements; and an identifier for this installation that does not identify you or your phone. Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to Google, which Google says it keeps only temporarily. Google uses these metrics to measure performance and usage and to maintain and improve MediaPipe. They never include audio, clips or sleep data: MediaPipe does not send the sound it analyzes to Google.

5. Ads and Google AdMob

Rested is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Home and Trends screens and, now and then, a full screen ad when you come back to Home or Trends after finishing something, such as logging a night or leaving the Sleep Coach. Ads never appear while you track a night, on the alarm screens, at bedtime (from an hour before your target bedtime until your target wake time), while sleep sounds are loaded, on the Sounds or Settings screens, or in the first 20 minutes after a night ends. Ads always start muted. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: your phone's advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as the phone model, Android version, language and app version;
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data.

Google states that the Mobile Ads software encrypts this data in transit. Rested never passes sleep, audio or health data to the ad software: ad requests carry no information about your nights. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Rested shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent software stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, then Ads; the exact place varies by phone).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

7. Notifications, alarms and overnight tracking

Rested creates its notifications on your phone: the bedtime wind-down reminder, the Morning Report reminder, the notification shown while a night is tracked, the controls for sleep sounds that are playing, and the smart alarm. With your permission it uses Alarms and reminders to ring the backup alarm on time, and full-screen notifications to show the alarm over your lock screen. While you track a night, Rested runs a foreground service and keeps the processor awake so tracking continues with the screen off; while sounds play, it runs a media playback service. None of this sends data anywhere.

8. Permissions

  • Microphone: optional, to detect snoring, coughs and sleep talk during a night you start. Without it Rested tracks with motion only.
  • Notifications: for reminders, the tracking notification and the smart alarm.
  • Alarms and reminders: optional, so the backup alarm and the wind-down reminder ring on time.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the alarm over your lock screen.
  • Foreground services, keeping the phone awake and motion sensor sampling: to track a night with the screen off and to play sleep sounds.
  • Run at startup and vibration: to restore your alarm and reminders after a restart, and for the alarm.
  • Internet and network state, advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob, Google's consent form and MediaPipe's statistics as described above.

Rested does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, photos, files or phone calls.

9. Backups and a new phone

Rested turns off Android's cloud backup for its data, so nothing from Rested is stored in your Google account. On Android 12 and later, if you move to a new phone with Android's phone to phone transfer, your Rested data, including clips, can be copied directly to the new phone. On Android 11 and earlier it is not copied. Export your data (section 10) to keep your own copy.

10. Exporting and deleting your data

In Settings, then Export or delete data, you can export all of your nights and your profile as a JSON file that you choose where to send, and you can permanently delete everything Rested stored on your phone, including clips, reminders and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Your answer to Google's consent form is kept by Google's software on your phone; change it in Settings, then Privacy choices. Files you exported or clips you shared stay wherever you sent them.

11. Selling and sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We never share your sleep, audio or health data, and we never use it for advertising.
  • Apart from the advertising information Google collects and MediaPipe's statistics described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.
  • We do not offer or require accounts or sign in, and we do not add analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

12. Not a medical device

Rested provides sleep estimates for general wellness only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your sleep or health, talk to a qualified healthcare provider.

13. Children

Rested is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

14. Security

Your information stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock turned on. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

16. Contact

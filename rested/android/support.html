Help & support

Rested: Sleep Tracker & Coach for Android. Effective date: October 23, 2026

Need help with Rested for Android, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com with a short description and, if you can, your phone model and Android version. We usually reply within 1 to 2 days.

How should I place my phone?

Put it face down on the mattress near your pillow, plugged in if you can. Rested reads gentle movement and, if you allow it, sound. It works through a fitted sheet.

Does tracking use a lot of battery?

A full night uses some battery, so charge your phone while you track. Rested reminds you to plug in when the battery is under 30% and not charging; you can turn that reminder off in Settings, then Tracking & detection.

Tracking stopped during the night

Some phones stop apps overnight to save battery. In Settings, then Tracking & detection, tap Open battery settings and set Rested's battery usage to Unrestricted. A night that was interrupted is saved up to the last moment Rested recorded, the next time you open the app.

Where do snore recordings go?

Short clips are stored only in Rested's private storage on your phone. You can play them in your Morning Report, and they leave your phone only if you tap Share clip. They are deleted with their night and with Delete all data, and clips older than 14 days are removed the next time Rested listens.

The smart alarm did not ring

The smart alarm needs a night that you started in Rested and notifications allowed for Rested. In Settings, then Permissions, also allow Alarms and reminders, so the backup alarm can ring on time, and Alarm over lock screen. Check that your alarm volume is up. The alarm rings no later than the end of your wake window.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep Rested free, with every feature included. A banner can appear above the tab bar on the Home and Trends screens, and now and then a full screen ad when you come back to one of them after finishing something. Ads never appear while you track a night, on the alarm screens, at bedtime, while sleep sounds are loaded, on the Sounds or Settings screens, or in the first 20 minutes after a night ends, and they always start muted. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in Settings, then Privacy choices.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. In Settings, then Export or delete data, Export all data (JSON) gives you a complete file of your nights and profile. You can also delete everything from the same screen.

Is Rested a medical device?

No. Rested gives sleep estimates for general wellness. If you are worried about your sleep, snoring or breathing at night, please talk to a doctor.

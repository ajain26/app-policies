Privacy Policy

ScanCraft for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

ScanCraft for Android ("the app") is a QR code and barcode scanner and creator, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the codes you scan and create and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your scans, the codes you create and your settings stay on this device and are never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them. Two other features use the internet: the product lookup for retail barcodes and Google's code reading diagnostics, both described below.

1. Who we are

ScanCraft for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your scans and codes stay on this device

ScanCraft keeps the following only on this device, in its private storage:

  • History: the codes you scan, import or type (their content, type, format and time), while Save in history is on in Settings.
  • My Codes: the codes you create, with their pictures.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as vibration, sound and appearance.

None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data, there is no account and no sign in, and we use no analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

3. Camera

The app asks for the camera to scan codes. The camera runs only while the Scanner or Batch screen is open. Camera frames are read on this device to find codes; they are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can deny the camera and still scan a photo or type a code number.

4. Photos and your gallery

  • Import: the Android photo picker gives the app only the photo you choose. It is read on this device to find a code, and it is not stored or uploaded. The app has no permission to read your photo library or files.
  • Save to gallery (Android 10 and later): when you tap it, the app saves a picture of the code to Pictures/ScanCraft on this device. Nothing is saved there otherwise.

5. Code reading (Google ML Kit)

Codes are found and read on this device by Google ML Kit barcode scanning, which is built into the app. Camera frames, photos and the content of your codes are not sent to Google or to us. To keep the feature working, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage data over HTTPS: device information such as the model and Android version, the app's package name and version, identifiers for this installation of the app, performance metrics, the feature's settings and version, input and output sizes, event types and error codes. Google states that ML Kit does not transfer this data to third parties.

6. Product lookup (Open Food Facts)

When a result shows a retail barcode (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A or UPC-E), whether you scanned it, imported it, typed it in Write or opened it from History, the app looks the number up in Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), an open database of food and household products run by a non-profit association, to show the product's name, brand and picture. The request goes over HTTPS and contains:

  • the barcode number, and
  • the app's name and our contact address, in the form "ScanCraft/1.0 (Android; contact: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com)".

No identifier of you or your device and nothing else about the scan is sent. Like any internet request, it lets Open Food Facts see your IP address, and when a product picture is found it is loaded from Open Food Facts' servers too. No lookup is made for QR codes or other barcodes. Open Food Facts' privacy policy is at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

"Search the web" on a result opens a Google search for the barcode number in your browser, only when you tap it.

7. Links and other apps

When you open a link, call a number, write an email or a message, open a map, share a code, or add a contact, an event or a Wi-Fi network, the app hands that content to the app or system screen you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Links, including these legal pages, open in your browser.

8. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ScanCraft is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Create, History and Settings screens and, now and then, a full screen ad after you finish a task, such as closing a result opened from History or a code preview. Ads never appear on the camera screens. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device, and where they apply other identifiers tied to accounts signed in on the device
  • your IP address, which can indicate the general location of the device
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as taps and video views
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit using TLS. Ads never receive your scans or codes: nothing you scan or create is passed to the ad service. The app asks for ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower). We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

10. Google Play

The app may ask you once to rate it with Google Play's in-app review. That prompt is shown and handled by Google Play under Google's terms.

11. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include your History, your created codes with their pictures and your settings in that backup and restore them on a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices, temporary export files and whether the app already asked for the camera are not included.

12. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan codes. You can deny it.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, Google's privacy message, the product lookup and ML Kit's diagnostics.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Keep awake and foreground service: added by Google's ad libraries for their own background work. ScanCraft itself runs no foreground service.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, photo library or files. Adding a contact or an event opens your contacts or calendar app, where you decide what to save.

13. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects, the ML Kit diagnostics and the barcode numbers sent to Open Food Facts described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

14. Children

ScanCraft is meant for users 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

15. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Delete single scans in History and single codes in My Codes, or everything with Delete history and Delete my codes in Settings. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on this device. Files you shared or saved elsewhere, pictures saved to your gallery and copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them.

16. Security

Your scans and codes stay on this device, where they are protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.

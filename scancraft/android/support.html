Support

ScanCraft for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Questions or ideas? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and I read everything. You can also write from the app: Settings, Contact Support.

Where are my scans and codes stored?

Only on this device. Turn Save in history off in Settings to stop keeping scans, and use Delete history or Delete my codes there to remove them. History can export scans as CSV, TXT or JPEG files if you want a copy.

Why does ScanCraft ask for the camera?

To scan codes live. The camera runs only on the Scanner and Batch screens, and nothing it sees is saved or sent. Without the camera you can still use Import (a photo) and Write (type a code number).

A code will not scan. What can I try?

  • Fill the frame with the code, hold the phone steady and avoid glare.
  • Turn on the torch in low light (on phones with a flash).
  • Take a photo of the code and use Import.
  • For a retail barcode, type its number in Write.

Why is there no product information for my barcode?

Product details come from Open Food Facts, a community database of mostly food and household products, so many barcodes are not in it, and the lookup needs an internet connection. Use Search the web on the result to look the number up in your browser.

How do I move my scans and codes to a new phone?

Android backup and device transfer can bring your History, created codes and settings to a new phone (see the Privacy Policy). You can also export your History as a CSV file and keep it.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep ScanCraft free, with every feature included. A banner sits above the tab bar on the Create, History and Settings screens, and now and then a full screen ad appears after you finish a task. Ads never appear on the camera screens. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

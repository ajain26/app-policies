Which holidays does Kab Hai show for me?

It depends on who gives you holidays, which you chose when you started:

  • Central government office: the central government holiday list for Delhi and New Delhi, plus the two restricted holidays you pick in the Plan tab. Offices in other states may swap some days.
  • State government office: your state government's list.
  • Bank: the Reserve Bank of India holidays for your state, with the 2nd and 4th Saturdays and Sundays off.
  • Private company: a starter list of common holidays for your state, which you edit to match your company in My company holidays.
  • Student or other: Republic Day, Independence Day and Gandhi Jayanti, plus the festivals you mark as a day off.

What does Expected mean?

Some authorities notify next year's holidays late in the year. Until they do, Kab Hai shows the days it expects from the previous year's list, marked Expected. When the official list is published, an app update replaces them.

Why does a festival say Date may vary?

Dates come from panchang and official calendars. Festivals that depend on the moon sighting, or that fall on different days in different calendars and regions, can move by a day, so the app marks them Date may vary and explains why on the festival page.

A date looks wrong. How do I report it?

Open Settings, Report a wrong date. Your email app opens with a message that already has the app version and your profile; add the festival or holiday, the date the app shows and, if you can, a link to the official notification, then send it. You can also write to anshul.jainsm@gmail.com. Confirmed errors are corrected in a later update.

How do I change my state or weekly off?

Settings, Profile. Your planned leave, checklists and followed festivals stay as they are.

How does the leave planner work?

In the Plan tab, choose how many leave days you have and a range. Kab Hai looks for short gaps between holidays and weekly offs and ranks the ways to bridge them by the days off you get for each leave day. Add to my plan saves the leave days; My plan groups them into breaks. Check your employer's leave rules before you apply.

How do reminders work?

Turn them on in Settings, Reminders: the day before a holiday, the day before festivals you follow, and a long weekend tip three weeks ahead, all at the time you choose. On Android 13 and later, allow notifications when the app asks. If reminders do not arrive, check that notifications for Kab Hai are on in Android settings and that battery saving is not stopping the app. Reminders can arrive a few minutes late; Kab Hai does not use exact alarms.

Where is my data stored?

Only on this device. There is no account and no cloud copy, and Android backup is not used, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Kab Hai free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on the main screens, and now and then a full screen ad appears after you finish something, such as adding leave to your plan. There are no ads on festival pages, in the wishes you share, in Settings or in notifications. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

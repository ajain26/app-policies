Privacy Policy

Kab Hai for Android. Effective date: 25 October 2026

Kab Hai? Festivals & Holidays for Android ("Kab Hai", "the app") is a personal holiday and festival planner for India, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: everything you enter stays on this device, there is no account, the app has no analytics of its own, and it is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Kab Hai is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer in India. Kab Hai is not affiliated with any government. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What you enter stays on this device

The app stores what you tell it only on this device, in the app's private storage: your profile (who gives you holidays, your state or union territory and your weekly off), your planned leave, the restricted or company holidays you pick or edit, the festivals you follow, your festival checklists and your reminder settings.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The holiday lists and festival dates are bundled inside the app, so looking up a date sends nothing anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kab Hai is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Home, Calendar, Festivals and Plan screens and, now and then, a full screen ad after you finish something, such as adding leave to your plan. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps, and there are no ads on festival pages, in the wishes you share, in Settings or in notifications. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device or advertising identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app interactions, such as app launches and how you interact with ads, for example views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive what you entered: your profile, planned leave, checklists and followed festivals are not passed to the ad service. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform consent SDK) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

5. Notifications are optional

Reminders are optional: the day before a holiday, the day before festivals you follow, and a long weekend tip three weeks ahead. Nothing is reminded until you turn a reminder on in Settings or tap Remind me on a festival. Reminders are planned and shown by this device, and nothing about them is sent to us. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications the first time you turn a reminder on; you can deny it, or change it later in Android settings.

6. Sharing wishes and cards

When you share a wish or a festival card, Android's share sheet sends it only to the app and the people you choose. Nothing is shared automatically. Once something leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled.

7. Emails you send us

If you use Report a wrong date or write to us, your email app opens with a message you can read and edit before you send it. It includes the app version, your Android version and your profile (holiday giver, state and weekly off) so we can check the right list. We use what you send only to answer you and to correct the data, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

8. Android backup and device transfer

Kab Hai keeps its data out of Android backup and device to device transfer, so your profile and plans are not copied to your Google account and a new phone starts fresh.

9. Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders described above. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Internet and network state: only for ads and Google's privacy message. The app has no online features of its own.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos or files.

10. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

11. Children

Kab Hai is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you remove it. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; either removes everything the app stored on this device. Emails you sent us are deleted on request.

13. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

15. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

RemoteNest: Smart TV Remote  ·  Last updated: November 2026

1. The short version

RemoteNest has no account and no advertising, and the app talks to your TV over your own Wi-Fi. From version 1.3 it sends anonymous usage and crash reports, described in section 2, which you can turn off in the app. It does not collect your name, email, contacts or photos.

2. Usage and crash reports

From version 1.3, RemoteNest uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to learn which features are used and what goes wrong. They receive:

  • Which screens and buttons are used, which brand of TV is connected, and whether connecting or opening a TV app worked.
  • The text of voice commands, for example "play guitar on YouTube", and whether the app understood and carried them out. Text you dictate into the TV keyboard is never sent.
  • Purchase events: which plan, and whether the purchase completed.
  • Crash reports and basic device information, such as the iPhone model and system version.
  • An installation identifier created by Firebase, and a general location (country or city) that Google derives from your network address. The advertising identifier is not used.

This information is not linked to your name or to an account, is not used for advertising and is not sold. You can turn the reports off in the app: open Settings, then Privacy Report, and switch off "Usage and crash reports". Google's Privacy Policy explains how Google handles this information.

3. What stays on your iPhone

To work, RemoteNest keeps a few things on your device only:

  • The TVs you connect: their name, brand, network address and, for power-on, their network hardware address.
  • Pairing keys that your TV gives the app after you approve it on the TV.
  • Your settings, such as theme, haptics and remote layout.
  • Recent text you typed with the keyboard feature, so you can resend it. Avoid typing passwords there; you can clear this history in the app.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. Local network

RemoteNest asks for Local Network access only to find and control TVs and Chromecast devices on your Wi-Fi. Commands go directly from your iPhone to your TV and never pass through our servers. One exception: when you ask to play something by name on YouTube, the words you asked for are sent to YouTube to find the video, and YouTube tells its app on your TV to play it.

5. Photos and videos

When you cast, you choose photos or videos with the system photo picker. RemoteNest streams them from your iPhone directly to your TV or Chromecast over your Wi-Fi for the length of the cast. We never upload, keep or look at them.

6. Siri and Shortcuts

Siri phrases and Shortcuts run on your device and send the matching command to your TV.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Premium is unlocked.

8. Third-party software

RemoteNest includes the Google Cast SDK to find and play on Chromecast devices. From version 1.3 it also includes Google's Firebase SDKs for the reports described in section 2. Google's Privacy Policy governs anything those SDKs handle. RemoteNest contains no advertising SDKs.

Versions before 1.2 sent anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck. Versions 1.2 to 1.2.2 sent none.

9. Children's privacy

RemoteNest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

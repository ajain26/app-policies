Privacy Policy

RemoteNest: Smart TV Remote  ·  Last updated: November 2026

1. The short version

RemoteNest does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks to your TV over your own Wi-Fi, and we run no servers that receive information about you or how you use the app.

2. What stays on your iPhone

To work, RemoteNest keeps a few things on your device only:

  • The TVs you connect: their name, brand, network address and, for power-on, their network hardware address.
  • Pairing keys that your TV gives the app after you approve it on the TV.
  • Your settings, such as theme, haptics and remote layout.
  • Recent text you typed with the keyboard feature, so you can resend it. Avoid typing passwords there; you can clear this history in the app.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Local network

RemoteNest asks for Local Network access only to find and control TVs and Chromecast devices on your Wi-Fi. Commands go directly from your iPhone to your TV and never pass through our servers.

4. Photos and videos

When you cast, you choose photos or videos with the system photo picker. RemoteNest streams them from your iPhone directly to your TV or Chromecast over your Wi-Fi for the length of the cast. We never upload, keep or look at them.

5. Siri and Shortcuts

Siri phrases and Shortcuts run on your device and send the matching command to your TV. We receive nothing.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Premium is unlocked.

7. Third-party software

RemoteNest includes the Google Cast SDK to find and play on Chromecast devices. Google's Privacy Policy governs anything that SDK handles. RemoteNest contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Versions before 1.2 sent anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck. That was removed in version 1.2.

8. Children's privacy

RemoteNest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

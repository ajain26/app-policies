1. What Roz ki English is

Roz ki English teaches everyday English sentences to Hindi speakers: you read a sentence, build it from word tiles, hear it and, if you choose, say it aloud while your phone checks the words it heard. It plans short daily sets and brings sentences back for review. Learning works offline; ads, Google's privacy message and a few usage events need internet. Roz ki English is an independent app and is not affiliated with any school, exam board, employer or publisher. It is a practice tool: it makes no promise about fluency, exams, jobs or any other result.

2. The sentences

The sentences, their Hindi meanings and their notes are written and checked by us, but they may still contain errors, and English can often be said in more than one correct way. The speaking check matches the words your phone heard; it is not a pronunciation score and can mishear. If you find an error, or think your answer was right, use "गलती दिखे? बताएँ" or "मेरा जवाब भी सही है? बताएँ" in the app, or email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com; confirmed errors are fixed in a later update.

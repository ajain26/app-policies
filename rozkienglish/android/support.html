How does a set work?

A set has up to 10 sentences. Each new sentence first comes as a card with its Hindi meaning, the English sentence and its words. Then you build every sentence from word tiles and tap "जाँचें". Sentences you learned come back after 1, 2, 4, 8 days and longer, so they stick. Closing a set keeps your progress; it continues where you left it.

Does it need internet?

No. All sentences are inside the app and there is no sign in. Only ads, Google's privacy message and a few usage events use the internet, and the English voice only if your phone has no voice installed.

How does speaking practice work?

After you build a sentence, the app can ask you to say it. If you choose "हाँ", allow the microphone; your phone's own speech recognition then shows which words it heard. Your voice is never recorded or sent anywhere. On phones without on-device English recognition, or if you do not allow the microphone, you hear the sentence and repeat it yourself. You can change this, or check again with "फिर से जाँचें", in Settings.

I cannot hear the English sentence.

The app uses your phone's text to speech voice. If none is installed, the app offers the phone's voice install screen once; you can also install an English voice in your phone's settings, under text to speech. Without a voice, everything still works as text.

How do I keep my progress safe, or move it to a new phone?

In Settings, open "बैकअप फ़ाइल" and choose "बैकअप फ़ाइल बनाएँ". Save the file in Downloads or Google Drive, or send it to yourself. On the new phone, install Roz ki English, open the same screen and choose "फ़ाइल चुनें" under "बैकअप फ़ाइल से वापस लाएँ". The app shows the file's date and learned sentences next to the phone's before it replaces anything, and you can undo with "वापस करें" for up to 7 days, or until you save anything new. The file holds your progress in readable form, so keep it private. Android's own Google backup may also bring your progress back on a new phone, but the file is the dependable way.

A sentence looks wrong, or my answer was marked wrong but I think it is right.

On the sentence card use "गलती दिखे? बताएँ", and on an answer use "मेरा जवाब भी सही है? बताएँ". Each opens your email app with a message that already names the sentence and the app version; add what you think and send it. Confirmed errors are fixed in a later update.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Roz ki English free. Banners appear on the home, situations and progress screens; a full screen ad can appear after a finished set; a reward ad plays only when you choose it to keep your streak. No ad appears while you learn. If an ad seems wrong, use "अनुचित विज्ञापन बताएँ" in Settings. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

How does the reminder work?

Choose a time in Settings, under "रोज़ याद दिलाएँ". On Android 13 and later, allow notifications when the app asks. The reminder can arrive a few minutes late, is skipped on days you already met your goal, and is dropped if the phone delivers it more than 2 hours late.

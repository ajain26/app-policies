Privacy Policy

Roz ki English for Android. Effective date: 8 November 2026

Roz ki English ("the app") helps Hindi speakers practise spoken English with everyday sentences. It works offline and is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your learning stays on your phone and there is no account. If you turn on speaking practice, your voice is understood by your phone itself and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them, and it records a few usage events with Firebase Analytics that never contain the sentences you learn or anything you say.

1. Who we are

Roz ki English is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer in India. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your learning stays on your phone

The app saves what you do only on your phone, in the app's private storage: which sentences you have learned, when each one comes back for review and how often you got it right, the sentences you saved, your unfinished and recent sets with the answers you built, your daily totals and streak, the days you kept with a streak ad, and your settings (Hindi form, starting point, daily goal, reminder time, speaking choice, the "ऐसे बोलें" line and voice speed).

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no server that receives your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • All sentences are bundled inside the app, so learning needs no internet. The internet is used only for ads, Google's privacy message, the usage events in section 7 and, if your phone needs them, the voice and speech services in sections 3 and 4.

3. The microphone and speaking practice

Speaking practice is optional and off until you choose it. The app asks for microphone permission only when you say yes to speaking practice, in a set or in Settings. You can say no, or turn speaking off in Settings, and everything else keeps working.

  • Your voice is understood only by your phone's own on-device speech recognizer (Android 12 and later). The app never uses a recognizer that may send audio to a server, and on phones without on-device recognition it uses listen and repeat instead, without the microphone.
  • Audio is never recorded, never stored and never sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not keep a copy of your voice anywhere.
  • The words the phone heard are kept only in the phone's memory while the speaking step is open, to show which words matched. They are cleared when the step closes and are never saved, never logged, never added to a backup and never sent to analytics.
  • Speaking never changes your score, your review schedule or your streak.
  • If your phone first needs Android's English recognition model, the app offers "अंग्रेज़ी पहचान डाउनलोड करें". Only if you tap it does Android download that model, from Google, through Android itself.

4. The English voice

The app reads English sentences aloud with your phone's own text to speech engine. It prefers a voice installed on the phone. Only if no installed English voice exists, and the phone is online, it may use a network voice of that engine; the engine then sends the English sentence being read, which is the app's own text and never anything you said or typed, to the engine's provider.

5. The backup file

When you choose "बैकअप फ़ाइल बनाएँ" (make a backup file), the app writes one file with everything listed in section 2. The file is readable text (JSON), so anyone who has the file can read your learning history and settings. It goes wherever you save or share it: the app opens Android's own file picker or share sheet, and you choose the place, for example your Downloads folder, Google Drive or another app. The app does not upload the file anywhere by itself, and we never receive it. Once the file is outside the app, the place or app you chose decides how it is kept. Keep it somewhere you trust.

When you restore from a file, the app reads only the file you pick. Before it replaces anything it keeps a copy of your current data inside the app, so you can undo the restore for up to 7 days, or until you save anything new; that copy stays on your phone and is deleted after that.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Roz ki English is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC:

  • a banner at the bottom of the home, situations and progress screens, in its own strip;
  • sometimes a full screen ad after you finish a set and tap "होम" or "एक और सेट": never during a set, never after your first set, never in the first day after you install the app, at most once every 3 minutes and at most 4 times a day;
  • a full screen reward ad, usually a video, only when you choose "विज्ञापन देखें" to keep your streak after a missed day.

No ad appears while you learn or build a sentence, during the first setup, in Settings or in a dialog, and there are no ads when you open the app. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device or other identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device, and where applicable identifiers of accounts signed in on the device
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location
  • app interactions, such as app launches and how you interact with ads, for example views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your learning history or anything you say. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

We block dating, gambling, get rich, loan and cleaner ad categories and allow ads rated PG at most. AdMob's filters cannot block every ad, so if you see an ad that should not be there, use "अनुचित विज्ञापन बताएँ" (report an inappropriate ad) in Settings; it opens your email app with a message naming the screen and the app version, and we act on reports.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how many people come back after a day, a week and a month, and records only these events of its own:

  • set_finished: a set was finished, whether it was the daily set or a practice set, and a rough score band (low, mid, high or full), never the score of a sentence;
  • speak_mode: how speaking practice works on the phone (on-device, listen and repeat, or off);
  • backup_made: a backup file was made;
  • restore_done: a backup file was restored.

It never records the sentences you learn or save, their ids, your answers, or anything the phone heard. Automatic screen reporting is turned off; advertising personalisation signals are turned off; and Analytics does not collect the advertising ID. Firebase also records standard information automatically, such as app opens and sessions, the app version, Android version, device model, language, a coarse location derived from a masked IP address, and an app instance identifier that is not your name or email.

The app sets Firebase's consent itself, after every check with Google's consent tool: analytics storage is allowed only where the consent tool reports that consent is not required, and stays denied where consent is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland); ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always denied for Analytics. Firebase Analytics is run by Google and follows Google's privacy terms at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Your choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (the User Messaging Platform consent tool) on the home screen before personalised ads are enabled, and Settings then shows Privacy choices (निजता के विकल्प), where you can review or change your choice at any time. A change applies at once. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalised ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalised.
  • Speaking practice, the reminder and the voice speed are your choice in Settings.

9. Location

The app never asks for location permission and never reads your phone's location. Google's ad and analytics SDKs can still estimate an approximate location, such as the city or region, from your IP address, as described in sections 6 and 7.

10. The reminder is optional

The daily reminder is planned only if you choose a time, during the first setup or in Settings. It is planned and shown by your phone, and nothing about it is sent to us. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications when you choose a reminder; you can deny it, or change it later in Android settings. The reminder names today's situation and never carries ads, streak warnings or promotions.

11. Reporting a mistake

The report buttons in the app ("गलती दिखे? बताएँ" and "मेरा जवाब भी सही है? बताएँ" on a sentence, and the reports in Settings) only open your email app with a message you can read and edit before you send it. It names the sentence or screen, the content version, the app version and, for an answer report, the answer you built. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

12. Android backup and a new phone

The app allows Android backup for only the app's database: your learning progress, saved sentences, sets and settings. Ad data, the consent tool's storage, the time of your last backup file and the app's restore copy are never included. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can copy that database to your Google Drive backup and restore it when you set up a new phone with the same account. This is done by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot see the backup. It is a bonus, not a promise; the backup file in section 5 is the dependable way. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's Google settings.

13. Permissions

  • Microphone: only for speaking practice, after you choose it, as described in section 3.
  • Notifications: only for the daily reminder, once you choose a time.
  • Run at startup: only to plan the daily reminder again after your phone restarts. If you have not chosen a reminder time, nothing is planned.
  • Internet and network state: only for ads, Firebase Analytics, Google's privacy message and, when needed, the voice and speech services of your phone. Learning works fully offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Permissions that the app's libraries add: wake lock and foreground service (Android's WorkManager, to plan the reminder in the background), Android's ad services permissions (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK), the Google Play install referrer (used by Firebase), and a permission private to this app that protects its own internal messages.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera or photos. It reads or writes a file only when you pick one in Android's file picker.

14. Software from other companies

These parts of the app come from Google and can send data as described above, in these versions:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (play-services-ads 25.2.0), for ads;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (user-messaging-platform 4.0.0), for the privacy message and Privacy choices;
  • Firebase Analytics (Firebase BoM 33.7.0), for the usage events.

The other libraries in the app (Android Jetpack and Kotlin libraries) work only on your phone and send nothing. The fonts are Mukta and Kalam, under the SIL Open Font License; the full list is under Licences in Settings.

15. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and analytics information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

16. Age

Roz ki English is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

17. Deleting your data

Your learning stays on your phone until you remove it. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; either removes everything the app stored on your phone. Backup files you made stay wherever you saved them until you delete them. If Android backup is on, delete the backup from your Google account settings. Emails you sent us are deleted on request.

18. Security

Your learning stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled, and keep backup files somewhere private. The ad and analytics services (Google's SDKs) control how they send data, and Google states that they encrypt it in transit.

19. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

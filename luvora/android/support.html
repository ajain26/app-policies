Support

Luvora for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Questions, problems or feedback? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within one to two business days. Luvora for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps.

How many free messages do I get?

A small number every day, shown above the message box in every chat. They come back at midnight UTC; the app shows what time that is where you are. Messages that the app's safety screening answers itself never count.

What does Luvora Premium include?

More messages every day (up to 100), no ads, and memory notes that help your companions remember longer conversations. It renews automatically until you cancel in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.

I subscribed but still see the free limit or ads

  • Open Profile and tap Restore purchases, signed in to Google Play with the account you subscribed with.
  • A purchase that is waiting for approval or payment starts as soon as Google Play completes it.
  • If it still does not work, email us and include the order number from your Google Play receipt.

Why does a message say it was not sent?

  • Luvora needs an internet connection to write replies. Your message text is put back in the box so you can try again.
  • Free messages need this copy of Luvora to be installed from Google Play on a phone that passes Google Play's integrity checks.

Where are my chats stored?

Only on your phone. Messages are sent to our reply service and AI provider to write each reply but are not stored there. Deleting the app, or Profile, Delete all data, deletes your chats, and they cannot be recovered.

A reply was wrong or upsetting

Touch and hold the reply and choose Report. AI characters can make mistakes, and reports help us make Luvora safer. If you are going through something hard, please reach out to someone you trust or a crisis line: 988 in the US and Canada, Samaritans at 116 123 in the UK and Ireland, or findahelpline.com anywhere else.

Privacy Policy

Luvora for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Luvora for Android ("the app") lets you chat with original AI characters. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your chats, characters, memories and profile are stored on your phone. To write each reply, the messages of that chat are sent over an encrypted connection to our reply service and to our AI provider, only after you agree, and they are not stored on our servers. There are no accounts. If you do not subscribe, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Luvora for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Your profile (the name, pronouns, age range, interests and goal you enter), the characters you create, your conversations, the short memory notes kept for each conversation, the moments you save as Memories, and the replies you report are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. The app turns off Android backup and device to device transfer for this data, so it is not copied to a Google backup.

  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

3. Messages sent to write replies

Luvora's characters reply using an AI model that runs in the cloud, not on your phone. Before the first message is sent, the app shows what is shared and asks for your agreement; nothing is sent until you agree, and you can withdraw your agreement in Profile at any time (chatting then stops). When you send a message, the app sends over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our reply service (luvora-ai, which we run on Cloudflare Workers):

  • the recent messages of that conversation (up to the latest 40), including your new message
  • the character's name, description and example lines
  • the name, pronouns, interests and goal from your profile, if you entered them
  • for subscribers, the short memory note of that conversation

The reply service passes this to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, which writes the reply and sends it back. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models; Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Our reply service does not store your messages or the replies, and its logs record only the result, the time taken and the kind of request, never message text, tokens or identifiers.

Messages that the app's safety screening answers itself (for example a message about self harm, which is answered with crisis line information) are never sent anywhere.

For subscribers, the app also sends earlier parts of a long conversation to write its memory note, the same way.

4. Free messages and Google Play Integrity

Without a subscription, the app includes a small number of free messages each day. To count them and to keep the service from being abused, the app sends with each free message a random install ID created by the app (it is not linked to your name, Google account or phone number) and a Google Play Integrity token. Google Play services produce that token on your phone, and our reply service asks Google to decode it. The result tells us only whether this copy of the app was installed from Google Play, whether the phone passes Google's integrity checks, and that the token belongs to this exact message. To produce it, Google Play services process information about your phone and the app under Google's own privacy policy. Our reply service stores the install ID with a daily message count, and nothing else about you.

5. Subscriptions

Luvora Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm a subscription, the app sends its Google Play purchase token to our reply service, which asks Google Play whether the subscription is active. The service keeps a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the token, the date the subscription is paid up to, and a daily message count. It does not receive your name, email address or Google account.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not subscribe, the app shows ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the character list and the chat list and, now and then, a full screen ad when you leave a conversation. Ads never appear inside a conversation, in the character editor or during onboarding, and no ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. Subscribers see no ads, and the ads SDK is not started for them. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of your phone
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as taps and video views
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ads SDK

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. The app never passes your chats, characters, memories or profile to the ads SDK. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Profile then shows Privacy choices, where you can change your choice at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings.
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

8. Reporting a reply

You can report any AI reply by touching and holding it and choosing Report. The report (the reply's text, the character's name and the reason you chose) is saved on your phone, and the app opens your email app with a message to anshul.jainsm@gmail.com filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use reports only to improve the app's safety.

9. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to send messages to the reply service, to reach Google Play and, for non-subscribers, the Google ads and consent tools.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the subscription.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

10. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the messages sent to write replies (section 3), the Play Integrity and purchase checks with Google (sections 4 and 5) and the advertising information Google collects (section 6), the app shares nothing with third parties.

11. Adults only

Luvora is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

12. Deleting your data

Profile, Delete all data removes your chats, characters, memories, reports, profile and install ID from your phone, and deleting the app or clearing its storage does the same. Our reply service holds no chat text to delete. Its daily counts, keyed by the random install ID or by the hashed purchase token, cannot be linked to you by us. If you want them removed anyway, email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

13. Security

Everything the app sends travels over encrypted connections; the app refuses unencrypted ones. Your data on the phone is protected by the phone's own security, so keep a screen lock enabled.

14. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above before the change ships, in the app and on this page.

15. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I get a sound into Toneshop?

On the Tones tab, tap New tone, then choose Files for songs, voice memos and videos saved in Files, or Videos in Photos to use the sound from a video. You can also open Voice Memos or Files, tap Share on a recording, and choose Toneshop to bring it straight in.

How do I make it my ringtone?

Toneshop gets the file ready and your iPhone sets the ringtone. On iOS 26 and later, tap Use as Ringtone in the editor, then Share, then choose Use as Ringtone. Your new tone appears in Settings, under Sounds & Haptics. On earlier versions of iOS, Toneshop walks you through the last step in Apple's GarageBand app. You can open the guide any time from Settings, How to set a ringtone.

Why is my ringtone limited to 30 seconds?

iPhone ringtones and alert tones can be up to 30 seconds long, so Toneshop keeps your selection within that length. Tap Find the chorus to move the window to the part that repeats and hits hardest, then fine tune it with the handles.

Toneshop can't read my file

Toneshop works with M4A, MP3, WAV, AIFF and CAF audio, plus MOV and MP4 videos, up to 60 minutes long. Copy protected files cannot be used. A video with no sound track has nothing to turn into a tone. If a file you own still will not import, email us the file type and how you got it.

Which formats can I export?

Ringtone (M4R) and M4A files with every version. Toneshop Pro adds WAV, AIFF and CAF, a choice of bit rate and sample rate, converting up to 20 files at once, song info and cover art, and the Convert Audio action for Shortcuts.

How many exports can I make?

Without Pro you can export 2 files a day, counting editor exports, Use as Ringtone and each converted file. The count resets at midnight. Toneshop Pro removes the daily limit.

I restored my purchase and Pro is still locked

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Toneshop and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we will help.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toneshop, then Cancel Subscription. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase is a single payment and never renews.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. Toneshop contains no networking code at all. See the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Email us with your iPhone model, your iOS version, the type of file you used, and what you were doing when it happened. A description of the problem is enough; you do not need to send the audio itself.

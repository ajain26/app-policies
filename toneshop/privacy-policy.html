Toneshop

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

The short version: your audio never leaves your device. Toneshop has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of ours. The app contains no networking code at all.

Who we are

Toneshop: Ringtone Maker is an iOS app for turning sounds you own into ringtones, alert tones and audio files. It is developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us").

What stays on your device

The audio and video you bring into Toneshop, the tones you make, their names, edits and song info, and every file you export are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. All processing, including drawing the waveform and finding the chorus, happens entirely on your device. We operate no servers and have no ability to see, access, or recover any of it.

Because the data lives on your device, it is protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption, and it is included in your device backups if you have those enabled. Backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Photos, Files and other apps

Toneshop asks for no permissions. When you pick a video from Photos, Toneshop uses Apple's system picker, which gives the app only the video you choose and no access to the rest of your library. When you pick a file, or share one to Toneshop from Files, Voice Memos or another app, Toneshop receives a copy of that one file. Toneshop does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or notifications.

Shortcuts

The Convert Audio action for the Shortcuts app converts the files you give it on your device and hands the results back to your shortcut. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your data. The app has no networking code.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign in.
  • There are no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and no third party data sharing.
  • We do not sell or share any information about you, because we never receive any.

Purchases

Toneshop Pro is sold through Apple, as an auto renewing yearly subscription or a one time lifetime purchase. Payment, billing, and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details. We receive no personal information from Apple about who purchased.

Children

Toneshop is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting a tone in the app removes it and its imported audio from your device. Files you already saved to Files or shared elsewhere stay where you put them. Deleting Toneshop removes everything it stored. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

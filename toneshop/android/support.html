Where are my tones stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud, and deleting the app deletes your tones. Tones you added to your phone's sound list and files you saved elsewhere stay where they are. Android's cloud backup does not include your tones, but a device to device transfer to a new phone can copy them.

Which files work?

Most audio files your phone can play, such as M4A, MP3, AAC, WAV, OGG and FLAC, and the sound of MP4 and other videos. Pick them in Toneshop, or share them to Toneshop from another app. Sounds up to 60 minutes long can be used. Toneshop saves tones as M4A files and can also export or convert to WAV.

How do I set a ringtone?

  • Open a tone and tap Set as sound.
  • Choose Ringtone, Notification or Alarm.
  • Tap Set as default to use it right away, or Add to sounds only to add it to your phone's sound list and pick it later in your phone's settings.
  • The first time you tap Set as default, Toneshop explains that Android needs you to allow it to modify system settings, then opens that Android page. Turn on the switch for Toneshop and go back to finish.

I set it, but my phone still plays the old sound. What now?

Some phones keep their own sound settings, for example a separate ringtone for each SIM card or for a contact. Open your phone's Settings, then Sound, and pick the tone there: tones you added with Toneshop are in the list under the name you gave them.

Why can't I use a song from a streaming app?

Songs saved for offline listening in streaming apps are copy protected, and Toneshop cannot open copy protected files. Toneshop only edits audio and video you already have, and you should use sounds you own or have permission to use.

Is everything free?

Yes. Every feature is free and there are no in-app purchases. The app is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the tone list and Settings, and now and then a full screen ad when you come back to the tone list after finishing a tone. Ads never appear in the editor, during playback or in the Convert tab.

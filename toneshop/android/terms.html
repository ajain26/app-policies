1. What Toneshop is

Toneshop is an audio editing tool. It takes the sound of files you already have, lets you trim it, add fades and even out its volume, finds the part that repeats, and saves the result as a ringtone, notification sound, alarm sound or audio file. It also converts audio and video files to M4A or WAV. It works on your phone, without an account; ads need an internet connection.

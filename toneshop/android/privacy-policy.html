Privacy Policy

Toneshop for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Toneshop for Android ("the app") turns sounds and videos you already have into ringtones, notification sounds and alarm sounds, and converts audio files. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: the files you pick and the tones you make stay on your phone, all editing happens on your phone, and we run no servers and have no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Toneshop for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. The files you pick

Toneshop opens only the files you choose: a file you pick in Android's document picker, a video you pick in Android's photo picker, or a file you share to Toneshop or open with it from another app. Android gives the app access to that one file and nothing else. Toneshop copies the sound of it into the app's private storage on your phone, so your tone keeps working if the original moves. For a video, only its sound is kept. The app does not ask for permission to read your music, videos or other files.

3. Editing happens on your phone

Trimming, fades, volume, the waveform, the chorus finder, the preview and every export or conversion run inside the app, on your phone. Your audio is never uploaded and never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. The chorus finder is plain signal processing that compares the parts of the sound with each other; it works without an internet connection.

  • Your tones (their names, edit settings and waveforms) and the sound copied from your files are stored only in the app's private storage.
  • Exported and converted files are written to the app's temporary storage on your phone. They leave it only when you share them through Android's share sheet or save a copy where you choose, and the app or place you send them to decides how they are handled. The app clears its temporary files when it starts.
  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The app has no network code of its own. Only the Google ads and consent tools described below use the internet.

4. Setting a ringtone, notification or alarm sound

When you tap Set as sound, Toneshop adds a copy of your tone to your phone's sound list (the Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms folder) through Android's media store, so it appears where your phone lists its sounds. If you choose Set as default, Android first asks you to allow Toneshop to modify system settings; only with that allowed does Toneshop change your default ringtone, notification sound or alarm sound, and only to the tone you chose. Toneshop uses this access for nothing else, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings. On Android 8 and 9, adding a tone to the sound list asks for storage permission, used only to write that copy. Tones you add stay in your phone's sound list until you delete them there.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Toneshop is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the tone list and Settings and, now and then, a full screen ad when you come back to the tone list after finishing a tone. Ads never appear in the editor, during playback, in the Convert tab or during onboarding, and no ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of your phone
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. The app never passes your files, tones, audio or settings to the ads SDK, and ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • Modify system settings: only to set your chosen tone as the default ringtone, notification sound or alarm sound, when you tap Set as default. Android asks you to allow it in its own settings page, and Toneshop explains why before it opens that page.
  • Storage, on Android 8 and 9 only: to add a tone to your phone's sound list. Newer versions of Android need no permission for this.
  • Internet and network state: used only by the Google ads and consent tools.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

Each permission is optional. Without Modify system settings you can still add tones to your sound list and pick them in your phone's settings. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, camera or the music and videos on your phone.

8. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's settings (for example that you finished the welcome steps) in your device backup. Your tones and the sound copied from your files are never part of the cloud backup. When you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer, Android can copy the app's data, your tones included. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings.

9. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

10. Children

Toneshop is a general-audience tool for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Deleting your data

Deleting a tone in the app deletes it and the sound copied for it. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all your tones. Tones you added to your phone's sound list and files you shared or saved elsewhere stay where they are until you delete them there. There is nothing for us to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

12. Security

Your information stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above before the change ships, in the app and on this page.

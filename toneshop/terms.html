What Toneshop does

Toneshop lets you bring in a sound, trim it, add fades, even out its volume, preview it, and export it as a ringtone, an alert tone or an audio file. It can suggest the part of a song that repeats and hits hardest. It can also convert audio and video files to other audio formats, in the app and through the Convert Audio action for Shortcuts.

Use sounds you own

Only use audio and video that you own or have permission to use. You are responsible for the content you import, edit, export and share. Toneshop does not check who owns a recording. Copy protected files cannot be used.

Setting a ringtone

Toneshop prepares the file. Your iPhone sets the ringtone. On iOS 26 and later you hand the file to the system Use as Ringtone action from the share sheet. On earlier versions of iOS, Apple's GarageBand app does the last step, and Toneshop shows you how. Toneshop never changes your ringtone settings itself, and the availability of these Apple features is up to Apple.

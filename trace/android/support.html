Support

Trace for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Questions, problems or ideas? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within one to two business days. Trace for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps.

Why only one Daily Card a day?

The Daily Card is one card for the day, so it is drawn once per calendar day. The other spreads, Yes, No, Maybe and Past, Present, Future, can be drawn as often as you like.

How are the readings written?

Each card has its own text, written by hand, for upright and reversed, with a line for love, work, money and yourself. The app picks the parts that fit your spread, the seat the card sits in and the topic you chose, and adds a memory line when a card has come to you before. The same card in the same place always reads the same way. Nothing is sent anywhere to write it.

How does the deck notice a returning card?

Trace counts the cards in your journal. When the same card comes three times in thirty days, answers the same topic twice, or one suit fills more than half of your recent cards, it says so, with the plain odds of that happening by chance. Cards are always drawn fairly at random.

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud, and deleting the app deletes your journal. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back it up with your Google account.

I did not get a check-in notification.

  • Check-ins come for questions asked with Yes, No, Maybe or Past, Present, Future, three days later in the evening.
  • Make sure Check back on old questions is on in Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Trace in Android settings.
  • Android may deliver it a little late when the phone is saving battery.

Is everything free?

Yes. Every spread, reversed cards, the Ledger, the journal and check-ins are free. The app is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the Deck, the Ledger and the Journal, and now and then a full screen ad when you return to the Deck after a reading. There are no in-app purchases.

How do I start over?

Settings, Burn the ledger deletes everything the deck remembers and starts the app from the beginning. It cannot be undone.

Privacy Policy

Trace for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Trace for Android ("the app") is a tarot journal: you draw cards, read what each card says, and keep a private record of your readings. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your name, questions, readings, notes and settings stay on your phone, and we run no servers and have no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Trace for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The name you give the app, your tarot experience and focus topic, every question you ask, the cards you draw, their readings, whether a reading landed, your journal notes, your answers to check-ins and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, in one file. Readings are written on your phone by the app's own fixed text and rules; nothing is sent anywhere to create them. None of this is ever sent to us: we operate no servers and cannot see, access or recover it.

  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The app has no network code of its own. Only the Google ads and consent tools described below use the internet.
  • Your questions, readings and notes are never passed to the ads SDK.

3. Check-in notifications

When you ask a question with a deeper spread, the app can check back three days later with a notification, scheduled on your phone by Android. The notification never shows your question or your cards. You can turn check-ins off in Settings, or turn notifications off for the app in Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Trace is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the Deck, the Ledger and the Journal and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to the Deck after finishing a reading (at most one every five minutes). Ads never appear during onboarding, while you draw or read your cards, on a journal entry or in Settings, and no ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of your phone
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, on Android 13 and later: only if you allow it, for the check-ins described above.
  • Run at startup: so a scheduled check-in survives a restart of your phone.
  • Internet and network state: used only by the Google ads and consent tools.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's file (your name, questions, readings, notes and settings) in your device backup, and the same file can be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings.

8. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

9. Children

Trace is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Deleting your data

Settings, Burn the ledger deletes everything the app remembers on your phone. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, does the same. Copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them. There is nothing for us to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

11. Security

Your information stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

12. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above before the change ships, in the app and on this page.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on this device. Back up from Settings, Backup & Export, to keep a copy.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Save a backup in Settings, Backup & Export, copy the file to the new phone, install TradeBill there and choose the file with Choose Backup to Restore. Restoring replaces the data on the new phone.

How do I change my logo, tax rate or payment instructions?

Settings, Business Profile. Changes apply to new PDFs; documents you already shared keep their original details.

How do invoice reminders work?

TradeBill sends one quiet reminder the morning an unpaid invoice is due. Turn reminders on or off in Settings, Notifications.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep TradeBill free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on the main lists, and now and then a full screen ad appears when you return to a list after finishing a task. Ads never appear inside your estimates, invoices, PDFs or printouts. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

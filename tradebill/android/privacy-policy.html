Privacy Policy

TradeBill for Android. Last updated: October 23, 2026

TradeBill for Android ("the app") is an app for estimates and invoices, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: everything you enter stays on this device and is never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

TradeBill for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your business data stays on this device

Everything you enter is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: your business details and logo, clients, prices, estimates, invoices, payments, job photos, signatures, notes, properties, jobs, expenses with receipt photos, and time entries.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TradeBill is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Home, Documents, Clients and Price Book lists and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to a list after finishing a task. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your business data: no client, price, document or photo is passed to the ad service, and no ad is ever added to the PDFs you create, share or print. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

5. Receipt text (Google ML Kit)

When you add a receipt photo to an expense, its text is read on this device by Google ML Kit text recognition, which Google Play services downloads and runs. The receipt image and its text are not sent to Google or to us. To keep the feature working, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic data over HTTPS: device information such as the model and Android version, the app's package name and version, a device identifier, performance metrics, the feature's settings and error codes. Google states that ML Kit does not transfer this data to third parties.

6. Contacts, photos and notifications

  • Contacts: when you import a client, the system contact picker gives the app only the one name and phone number you choose. The app does not ask for permission to read your contacts.
  • Photos: the system photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose for a logo, a job or a receipt. The app does not ask for permission to read your photo library or use the camera.
  • Notifications: invoice reminders are planned and shown by this device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

7. Files you share, print, back up or export

PDFs you share or print, backups you save and spreadsheets you export go only where you choose, such as an email, a messaging app, a printer or a folder. Once a file leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled.

8. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include the app's data (your records, photos, signatures and settings) in that backup and restore it to a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock; on Android 8 to 11 Android's own backup rules apply. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices are not included. Android stops backing up an app whose data grows too large, so use Settings, Backup & Export to keep your own copy.

9. Permissions

  • Notifications: for invoice reminders. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, Google's privacy message and the ML Kit download.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library or files.

10. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects and the ML Kit diagnostics described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

11. Children

TradeBill is a business tool for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

12. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Use Delete All Data in Settings, Backup & Export, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; each removes everything the app stored on this device. Backups and files you saved elsewhere, and copies in a Google backup, stay until you delete them.

13. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

15. Contact

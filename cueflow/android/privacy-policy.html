Privacy Policy

CueFlow for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

CueFlow for Android ("the app") is a teleprompter that scrolls your script over the camera and follows your voice. It is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your scripts, imported documents, settings and recordings stay on your phone, your voice is turned into text inside the app and never leaves your phone, and we run no servers and have no accounts. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

CueFlow for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Scripts you write, paste or import, their titles and personas, and your prompter settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, in an on-device database and a settings file. Takes you record are first written to the app's private storage and then saved to your phone's gallery, in the Movies/CueFlow folder. None of this is ever sent to us: we operate no servers and cannot see, access or recover it.

  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The app has no network code of its own. Only the Google ads and consent tools described below use the internet.

3. Your voice stays on your phone

Voice-follow listens to the microphone only while the prompter is open and listening: while you rehearse in Voice mode, and while you film a take, in either mode, so you can switch to Voice mode during the take. A speech recognizer bundled inside the app (the open source Vosk recognizer with its small English model) turns the audio into text on your phone, purely to find your place in the script. The audio and the recognized text (the transcript) are held in memory only while the prompter follows you, are never stored, never leave your phone, are never uploaded, and are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. Voice-follow works without an internet connection.

4. Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera and the microphone only while the prompter is open: the camera to show the preview and film your take, and the microphone to record the take's sound and to follow your voice. Android shows its camera and microphone indicators whenever they are in use. The app never records in the background: when you leave the app during a take, the take stops and is saved.

5. Your recordings

Finished takes are saved to your gallery under Movies/CueFlow, where you can watch, share or delete them with any gallery app. CueFlow lists, plays, shares and deletes only the takes it created; it never reads your other pictures or videos. If a take cannot be saved, it stays in the app's private storage until you save, share or discard it. A take is shared only when you choose to share it, through Android's share sheet, and the app you send it to decides how it is handled.

6. Documents you import

When you import a script, Android's document picker gives the app only the one file you choose (a .txt, .md, .docx or .pdf file). The app reads its text once, on your phone, and saves it as a script. It keeps no link to the file and does not ask for access to your other files.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CueFlow is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the script list, Settings and the takes list and, now and then, a full screen ad when you come back to a list after finishing something (saving a script, leaving the prompter after a saved take, closing the player). Ads never appear on the prompter, while you record, in the editor, in the player or during onboarding, and no ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of your phone
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. The app never passes your scripts, imported documents, recordings, voice, transcripts or settings to the ads SDK, ads are never part of your videos, and ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

9. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to show the preview and film your takes, only while the prompter is open.
  • Microphone: to record the sound of your takes and to follow your voice, only while the prompter is open.
  • Storage, on Android 8 and 9 only: to save your takes to the gallery. Newer versions of Android need no permission for this.
  • Internet and network state: used only by the Google ads and consent tools.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

Each permission is optional. CueFlow explains why it needs the camera and the microphone before Android asks, and you can deny or revoke either one in Android settings: without the camera you can still rehearse, and without the microphone the script scrolls at your set speed and takes have no sound. The app does not ask for your location, contacts or the pictures and videos in your gallery.

10. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your scripts, your settings and the app's list of takes (each take's script title, date, length, size and its place in your gallery) in your device backup, and the same data can be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. The take videos themselves are not part of the app's backup (they are in your gallery), and neither are the speech model, the takes waiting to be saved or the app's record of which permissions it asked for. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings.

11. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

12. Children

CueFlow is a general-audience tool for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Deleting your data

Your scripts and settings live only on your phone. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them. Takes already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them, from the app's takes list or from any gallery app, and copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them. There is nothing for us to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

14. Security

Your information stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

15. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above before the change ships, in the app and on this page.

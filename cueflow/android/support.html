Support

CueFlow for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Questions, problems or feature requests? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within one to two business days. CueFlow for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps.

Where are my recordings saved?

In your phone's gallery, in the Movies/CueFlow folder, and in the app's takes list (the video button at the top of your scripts). CueFlow never uploads them or keeps a copy anywhere else. If a take could not be saved, CueFlow keeps it and offers to save, share or discard it.

Where are my scripts stored?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud, and deleting the app deletes your scripts, so copy anything important first. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back them up with your Google account.

Which files can I import?

Plain text (.txt and .md), Word documents (.docx) and PDF files with selectable text. Reading PDF text needs a recent version of Android; on older phones, copy the text and paste it into a new script instead.

Does voice-follow use the internet?

No. CueFlow recognizes your speech inside the app with a bundled on-device recognizer, so your voice never leaves your phone and voice-follow works without a connection. Ads and Google's privacy message are the only parts of the app that use the internet.

Is everything free?

Yes. Voice-follow, takes of any length, unlimited scripts, mirroring, 4K where your camera supports it and script import are all free. The app is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the script list, Settings and the takes list, and now and then a full screen ad when you come back to a list after finishing something. Ads never appear on the prompter or while you record, and there are no in-app purchases.

Voice-follow isn't tracking well. What helps?

  • Read at a natural pace in a quiet room, and keep the phone's microphone uncovered.
  • Make sure CueFlow has microphone access: Settings, Permissions.
  • Voice-follow understands English; other languages scroll better at a fixed speed.
  • Write numbers as words in your script ("twenty twenty six", not "2026"): voice-follow matches the words you say.
  • You can switch to fixed-speed scrolling at any time with one tap on the Voice button.

What if on-device speech is unavailable?

The first time you use CueFlow, it sets up its speech model on your phone, which needs about 75 MB of free space. Until it is ready, Settings, Voice-follow shows "Getting ready" and the prompter scrolls at your set speed. If there is not enough space, Settings shows "Unavailable" with a Try again button. You can always rehearse and film at a fixed speed.

Does voice-follow work while I film?

Yes, on Android 10 and later. On Android 8 and 9, voice-follow works while you rehearse, and takes scroll at your set speed.

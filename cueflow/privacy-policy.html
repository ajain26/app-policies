Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

The short version: your scripts stay on your device, your voice is recognized on-device and never leaves your iPhone, and your recordings are saved only to your own Photos library. The one exception is the optional AI script writer: if you choose to use it, the topic and details you type are sent to our server and to OpenAI to write your script, and are not stored by us. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Who we are

CueFlow is an iOS teleprompter app that follows your voice while you record, developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Your scripts stay on your device

Scripts you write, paste, or import, along with your app settings and persona choice, are stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData. They are never transmitted to us, and we have no ability to see, access, or recover them. A script the AI writer drafts for you is stored the same way once you save it.

Voice recognition runs on-device

CueFlow's voice-follow feature uses Apple's Speech framework configured for on-device recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition). Your speech is transcribed transiently, purely to keep the script scrolling at the reading position, and is then discarded. It is never stored, never turned into captions, and never leaves your iPhone. There is no cloud transcription and no server involved. If on-device recognition isn't available for your language, the prompter simply falls back to fixed-speed scrolling.

The optional AI script writer

CueFlow can draft a script for you with AI. This is the only part of the app that uses the internet, and it only runs when you ask it to write a script. The first time you open it, CueFlow explains what is sent and asks for your permission. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

What is sent. When you tap Write, the app sends the topic and optional details you typed, plus the persona, length, and tone you picked, to our server. Our server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers, passes them to OpenAI's API, which writes the script and returns it to your iPhone.

How we count your scripts. To manage the free allowance and the daily Pro limit, each request also carries one of the following: a random install ID that the app generates (it is not linked to your name or Apple Account), optionally with an Apple DeviceCheck token; or, if you own CueFlow Pro, Apple's signed record of your Pro purchase, which includes a transaction ID but not your name, payment details, or Apple Account.

What we keep. We do not store or log your topics, details, or scripts. Our server keeps only counters: how many free scripts each install ID has used, how many scripts each Pro purchase has written today, and a daily total.

OpenAI. Under OpenAI's API terms, requests are not used to train its models and may be retained for up to 30 days to detect abuse, after which they are deleted. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

If you never use the AI script writer, nothing is sent.

Camera, microphone, and your recordings

When you record, CueFlow uses the camera and microphone to capture your take. The resulting video is saved only to your own Photos library (with add-only access, so CueFlow cannot read your existing photos). We never receive, upload, or store a copy of your footage. What you do with a saved video afterward is entirely up to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts, logins, or registration.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not send your voice, recordings, or saved scripts to any server. Only the text you type into the AI script writer is sent, and only when you ask it to write a script.
  • We do not sell or rent your information. The only party it is shared with is OpenAI, as described above, solely to write your script.

Purchases

CueFlow Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app checks your purchase status on-device using Apple's StoreKit framework. If you use the AI script writer as a Pro owner, the app sends Apple's signed purchase record to our server so it can confirm that you own Pro. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app may ask for access to your microphone and speech recognition (for voice-follow), your camera (to record), and add-only access to your photo library (to save takes). Each is optional and explained on a CueFlow screen before the system prompt appears. CueFlow requests no other permissions.

Children

CueFlow is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your data

Your scripts and settings live only on your device. To erase them, delete the app, and iOS removes its entire local database. Recordings you already saved to Photos remain in your Photos library until you delete them there. The AI writer's usage counters are tied only to a random install ID or a purchase transaction ID, never to your name; to have yours removed, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before the change ships. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Privacy Policy

Vocabword for Android. Last updated: October 19, 2026

Vocabword ("the app") is a vocabulary app for Android, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your words, progress, lists and settings are stored on your phone, and the app has no account and sends nothing to us. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them. When you play a word's pronunciation, that word is sent to the Free Dictionary API to fetch a recording.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you do in Vocabword is kept in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Learning progress: the words you have seen, your review schedule, quiz and puzzle results, your streak and streak freezes, your daily goal and your Vocab Score history.
  • Your content: the word lists you make, the words you add yourself, and the words you bookmark, favorite, mute or mark as difficult or known.
  • Settings and welcome answers: the name you choose to be greeted by (optional), how you heard about Vocabword, your level, topics, theme, voice, app icon, appearance and reminder settings.
  • App open times: the time of day of your last 14 visits, so a smart reminder can arrive when you usually learn.
  • Reminder schedule: which reminders are planned and which were shown today.
  • The word shown on the home screen widget, and pronunciation recordings you have already played (section 5).

We do not run servers that receive this information, the app has no account or sign in, and it uses no analytics or crash reporting service of our own.

2. Deleting your data

Settings > Reset all data (Settings is the gear on the Profile tab) erases your progress, streak, lists, the words you added and your settings on this device, cancels your reminders and starts Vocabword from the beginning. This cannot be undone. Pronunciation recordings stay in the app's cache until you clear the cache in your phone's app settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Copies in a Google backup are managed by Google (section 3).

3. Device backup and transfer

If Android backup is turned on, your progress, lists, the words you added and your settings can be included in your Google account backup and restored when you set up a new phone, and they can be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. The reminder schedule and the recordings cache are never included. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Vocabword is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the main screens and, now and then, a full screen ad between activities. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome steps. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information about the ads SDK, such as crash and performance data

Ads never see your word lists, your progress or the words you add. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Pronunciation recordings (Free Dictionary API)

When you tap a speaker button, or a listening question plays a word, the app sends that word, and nothing else from the app, over HTTPS to the Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev), an independent public service, and downloads the recording it lists. This includes words you added yourself. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address and basic connection details to that service, which handles them under its own practices. We do not receive these requests.

Recordings are saved in the app's cache on your phone, so the same word plays later without a new request. When no recording is available or the service cannot be reached, your phone's text to speech engine reads the word instead. Definitions, examples and synonyms are built into the app and need no connection.

6. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app shows Google's privacy message before ads are personalized, and Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings under Privacy, Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show non personalized ads.

7. Reminders and notifications

Daily word and streak reminders are planned and shown by your phone. Vocabword uses no push notification service, and no reminder content leaves your device. You can change or turn off reminders in Settings > Notifications & Reminders, or in your phone's notification settings.

8. Home screen widget

The Word of the Day widget shows a word from the app on your home screen. It uses only the app's own data on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

9. Google Play and other apps

  • Vocabword may ask you to rate it with Google Play's in-app review, and Leave us a review opens the Play Store. Both are handled by Google Play.
  • Learn More opens a Google search for the word in a browser tab, handled by Google.
  • Sharing a word card, a quiz or puzzle result, or the app hands that content to the app you choose, and Give us feedback opens your email app with a message to us. That app's own privacy policy applies, and we receive only what you choose to send.
  • Saving a share card (Android 10 and later) puts the picture in your phone's Pictures/Vocabword folder.

10. Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders you choose. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, Google's privacy message and pronunciation recordings.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.

Vocabword does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Saving a share card uses Android's shared Pictures folder without a storage permission.

11. Purchases

Every feature is free. Vocabword has no in-app purchases, so the app never handles payment information.

12. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects and the pronunciation requests described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

13. Children

Vocabword is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

14. Security

Your information stays on your phone, where it is protected by your phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. Pronunciation requests use HTTPS, and Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it sends.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the latest version is always available in the app under Settings > Privacy Policy.

RoutineKit

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

The short version: your habits stay on your iPhone. RoutineKit has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers that receive your data.

Who we are

RoutineKit is an iOS habit tracker developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us").

What the app stores, and where

RoutineKit stores the habits you create (names, descriptions, icons, colors, categories), your completion history and streaks, reminder times, and your app preferences. All of it is kept only on your device, in the app's local database and in a container shared with the RoutineKit home screen widget. We operate no servers and cannot see, access, or recover this data.

Your data is included in your device backups if you have iCloud or computer backups turned on. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

How it is used

  • To track your habits and show your progress, streaks, and reports.
  • To schedule reminders as local notifications generated on your device.
  • To show your habits in the RoutineKit widget, which reads the same on-device data.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer accounts, logins, or registration.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. We never receive it in the first place.

Exports and feedback emails

Settings > Export Data creates a backup file that goes only where you choose to save or share it. If you send feedback from Settings, your email app opens with a message that includes basic device details (app version, device model, iOS version). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Purchases

RoutineKit is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete All Data permanently removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app from your device also removes its data (subject to any device backups you keep).

Children

RoutineKit is suitable for all ages. Because the app sends us no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

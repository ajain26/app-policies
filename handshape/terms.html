What Handshape does

Handshape helps you practice American Sign Language as used in the United States: the 26 letter fingerspelling alphabet, about 100 everyday signs in 8 units, a fingerspelling reading trainer, a spaced review queue and a Beta camera check of the handshape for 24 letters that hold still.

A practice aid, not a credential

Handshape is a study aid. It does not replace classes with a qualified ASL teacher or time spent with Deaf signers, and it does not certify your ASL skill. Signs can vary by region and community. The written sign descriptions in the current version are drafts that are waiting for review; the app marks them as drafts wherever they appear.

The camera check is a Beta

The camera check estimates whether your hand shape matches one of 24 still letters. It does not check movement, facial expression or full signs, and J and Z are not checked because they move. It can be wrong, especially for letters that differ mainly by the thumb. Treat its result as a hint to compare with the video, not as an assessment of your signing.

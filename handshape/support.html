Common questions

Where are the lesson videos?

Instructor videos are on the way. Until they arrive, each letter and sign shows a card that says "Instructor video coming", together with a written description of how to make it. The videos will appear in an app update with nothing for you to do.

Why are some descriptions marked "Draft, pending Deaf review"?

The written sign descriptions in this version are drafts. They are waiting for review by Deaf ASL instructors, and the app labels them so you know. If a description looks wrong to you, please email us with the name of the sign.

The camera check says camera access is off

Open the Settings app, scroll to Handshape and turn on Camera. Handshape only uses the camera while a camera check is running, and the video is never recorded or uploaded.

Why does the camera check skip J and Z?

J and Z are made with a movement, and the camera check only reads the shape of a hand that holds still. Copy the motion from the video for those two letters.

The camera check said "close enough". What does that mean?

A, E, M, N, S and T mostly differ by where the thumb sits, which the camera often cannot see. When your hand is in the right family of shapes, the check says close enough. Compare your thumb with the video.

How do I change my daily goal or writing hand?

Open the Settings tab in Handshape and use the Practice section. Your writing hand sets up the mirror view in lessons.

I bought Handshape Pro and it is still locked

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then open the Settings tab in Handshape and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we will help.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Handshape, then Cancel Subscription. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

How do I start over?

In the Settings tab, tap Reset progress. This clears your lessons, reviews, streak, trainer stats and camera results on this iPhone.

Reporting a bug

Email us with your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Handshape

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

The short version: Handshape has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and we run no servers. The app contains no networking code. Your progress stays on your iPhone, and camera video is never recorded or uploaded.

Who we are

Handshape: Learn ASL ("Handshape") is an iOS app for practicing American Sign Language, developed and operated by Anshul Jain ("we", "us").

What stays on your iPhone

Handshape keeps a small amount of learning data so it can plan your lessons and reviews:

  • the answers you give when you first open the app (why you are learning, how much ASL you know, what you want to start with, your daily goal and your writing hand);
  • which lessons you have finished and the review schedule for each letter and sign;
  • your daily practice count and streak;
  • your fingerspelling trainer level and scores;
  • for the camera check, which letters you checked and the best result for each.

All of it is stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. We operate no servers and cannot see, access or recover any of it. It is included in your device backups if you have those turned on; backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

The camera

The camera check is a Beta practice aid. It uses the front camera only after you tap "Start camera check" on a letter, and iOS asks for your permission the first time. While it runs, Apple's on-device Vision framework estimates the positions of your hand joints, and Handshape compares that hand shape with the letter you chose.

Camera video is never recorded, saved or uploaded. No photo or frame is stored. The only thing kept is the result for that letter. If you decline camera access, every other part of the app still works, and you can turn access on or off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit or store your data. The app has no networking code.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign in.
  • There are no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising and no third party data sharing.
  • Handshape does not use the microphone, your photos, your contacts, your location or notifications.
  • We do not sell or share any information about you, because we never receive any.

Purchases

Handshape Pro is sold through Apple as an auto renewing subscription or a one time purchase. Payment, billing and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details and receive no personal information from Apple about who purchased.

Children

Handshape is a general audience education app. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings, then Reset progress clears your lessons, reviews, streak, trainer stats and camera results. Deleting Handshape removes everything it stored. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

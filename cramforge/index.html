Cramforge

Point it at your notes. Walk in ready.

📸 Scan your own notes

Photograph your class notes or import a PDF. Cramforge reads the page on your iPhone.

📝 Real practice exams

Timed, scored practice questions built from your material, with a per-topic strength breakdown.

🔒 On-device & private

Questions are written by Apple's on-device model. No account, no upload, works in Airplane Mode.

⏳ Countdown study plan

A day-by-day plan sized to your material and your exam date, on your Lock Screen.

Cramforge is a self-testing tool for studying from material you already have. It does not solve homework and does not provide answers to assignments or exams.

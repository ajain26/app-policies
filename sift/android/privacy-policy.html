Sift for Android, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Sift: PDF Scanner & OCR for Android ("Sift" or "the app") is a document scanner published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). In short: your scans, the text Sift reads from them, your PDFs, your signature and your backups stay on your phone until you share, save, print or back up a file yourself. We run no servers and have no accounts, so we never receive your documents. Sift is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them. Google's scanner and text recognition may send Google diagnostic data, never your pages or their text. This policy explains the details.

1. Who we are

Sift for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your documents stay on your phone

Every page you scan or import, the text Sift reads from it, its title, category, vendor and dates, expiry dates and reminders, your favorites, your saved signature and your settings are stored only in Sift's private storage on your phone. Sift has no account and we run no server, so we cannot see, access or recover your documents.

  • There is no account, login or registration.
  • We do not run analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • Sift sends no document, page image, recognized text, title or signature to us or to any service.

3. Scanning and text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Sift scans with Google's document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services on your phone. Google Play services downloads the scanner once, in the background after you finish Sift's welcome screens, or when you first scan if it is not there yet. Sift reads the text on each page with Google ML Kit's text recognition, which works on your phone: the recognizer for Latin script is built into the app, and the optional recognizers for Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese and Korean, if you turn them on in Settings, are downloaded by Google Play services. Your page images and the text read from them are processed on your phone and are not sent to us or to Google.

To keep these features working, ML Kit and Google Play services may send Google diagnostic and usage data: device information such as the manufacturer, model, Android version and build; app information such as the package name and app version; per-installation identifiers, which are not meant to identify you or your device; and performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, event types and error codes. Google states that ML Kit encrypts this data in transit and does not transfer it to third parties: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

4. Importing pictures and files

Sift holds no camera permission and no permission to read your gallery or your files. You choose each picture in Android's Photo Picker, or each picture or PDF in Android's file picker, and Sift receives only what you choose. If the Google scanner is not available, Sift can open your camera app to take a photo of a page, and receives only that photo.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sift is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner at the bottom of the library and of Settings and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to the library after finishing something, such as saving a scan or sharing a PDF. Ads never appear inside your documents, your exported files or your prints, and never on the scanner, document, text or signing screens. No ad is requested before you finish the welcome screens. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

6. What the ads never receive

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Sift never passes your documents, page images, recognized text, titles, signature or backups to the ads SDK, and ad requests carry nothing about them. Ads are limited to Google's PG content rating. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Your ad choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Sift shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Without consent or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show non-personalized ads.

8. Sharing, saving, printing and exporting

Files leave your phone only when you share, print or save them yourself: a PDF, compressed PDF, picture or text file you send with Android's share sheet, save with Android's file creator, or print through Android's print service. Where they go then is up to you and the app or service you choose, under its own terms. A password protected PDF is encrypted with AES-256 using the password you set.

9. Encrypted backups

A Sift backup is one file encrypted with a password you choose (at least 8 characters for a new backup), saved wherever you pick. Sift never uploads it. Only that password opens the file, so choose a long one and keep it safe: we cannot recover the password for you, and Sift cannot recover the password either.

10. Expiry reminders and notifications

Sift asks to send notifications only when you turn on an expiry reminder, and sends them only for the reminders you turn on, 30, 7 and 1 day before a document's expiry date. Reminders are scheduled on your phone with Android's alarm service and scheduled again after the phone restarts, its clock or time zone changes, or Sift is updated; there is no push server. On the lock screen a reminder does not show the document's name.

11. Android backup, moving to a new phone, and uninstalling

Sift's documents, text and settings are not included in Android's cloud backup. They move to a new phone with your encrypted Sift backup file, or with Android's device to device transfer when the old phone runs Android 12 or later; on Android 8 to 11 that transfer copies nothing from Sift. Your ad choices are not included. Uninstalling Sift deletes every document it stored, unless you choose to keep its data when Android offers to, so make a backup first if you want to keep them.

12. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: only for the expiry reminders you turn on, asked when you turn the first one on.
  • Run at startup: to schedule your expiry reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads, Google's privacy message, the scanner download and Google's diagnostics described above. Sift contacts no server of its own.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

13. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described in section 5, and the diagnostics that ML Kit and Google Play services send Google as described in section 3, the app passes nothing to anyone. Your documents leave the phone only when you share, save, print or back them up.

14. Purchases

Sift has no in-app purchases. Every feature is free, so the app never handles payment information.

15. Children

Sift is a general audience productivity app for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

16. Your rights and deleting your data

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold none of your documents: you can view, edit, export or delete any of them in Sift at any time, and clearing Sift's storage in Android settings or uninstalling it erases them all. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them. For the data Google collects to show ads, use Privacy choices in Settings, your advertising ID settings in Android and the tools Google provides. There is nothing for us to delete on a server, because nothing was ever sent to us.

17. Security

Your documents stay in storage that only Sift can read, protected by your phone's own security. Backups and password protected PDFs are encrypted with your password. Every connection the app makes uses encrypted HTTPS; the app refuses unencrypted connections. On Android 13 and later Sift hides its pages from the recent apps screen.

18. Changes to this policy

If a new version of Sift changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version ships, and describe plainly what changed. The latest version is always in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy, and at https://ajain26.github.io/app-policies/sift/android/privacy-policy.html

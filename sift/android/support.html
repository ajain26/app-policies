Where do my scans go?

Into Sift's private storage on your phone, and nowhere else. Sift has no servers and no account, so nobody, including us, can see your documents. They leave your phone only when you share, print or save them, or put them in a backup file you save.

How does Sift name my documents?

When you scan a page, Sift reads the text on it on your phone, then works out the most title-worthy line, who the document is from, what kind of document it is and what date it carries. It fills in a title such as "Fernhollow Market · Receipt · Oct 2026" before you save, so you tap Save instead of typing a name. Every part of it can be edited if it guesses wrong.

The title it picked is wrong. Why?

Automatic titling reads whatever the scanner could see. Faint printing, unusual layouts, handwriting and glare all make it harder. Tap the title on the Review screen and type your own, or rename the document later from its menu. A cleaner, flatter, better lit scan usually helps the next one.

How do I find a document again?

Tap the search bar at the top of your library and type any word that appears inside the document, not just in its title. Sift searches the titles and the text it read from every page.

Can I start a scan from my home screen?

Yes. Touch and hold the Sift icon on your home screen, then tap Scan a document. The shortcut appears once you have finished the welcome screens.

Is the text in an exported PDF really searchable?

Yes. Sift writes the recognized text into every PDF as an invisible layer behind the page image, so you can select and search the text in other PDF apps instead of just looking at a picture of it. Corrections you make in Sift go into that layer too.

Is Sift free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no limits and nothing added to your pages. Sift shows a banner from Google AdMob at the bottom of the library and of Settings, and now and then a full screen ad when you return to the library after finishing something. There are no in-app purchases. Where the law requires it, Privacy choices in Settings changes your ad consent.

How do I protect a backup?

Tap Back up library in Settings and choose a password of at least 8 characters. The backup file is encrypted with it, and only that password opens it again, so keep it somewhere safe: nobody can recover it for you.

I got a new phone. Where are my documents?

Sift's documents are not part of Android's cloud backup. Tap Back up library in Settings, save the encrypted file somewhere your new phone can reach, then tap Restore from backup there. When the old phone runs Android 12 or later, Android's device to device transfer also brings your documents along as you set up the new phone from it.

My expiry reminder did not arrive.

Reminders need notifications to be allowed for Sift. If they are off, the document's menu offers Remind me before it expires again, which asks for them. Some phones also pause apps that have not been opened for a long time; Sift offers to keep its reminders working when you turn one on.

Can I lock Sift?

Sift has no lock of its own. Use your phone's screen lock, and an app lock or private space where your phone offers one. On Android 13 and later, Sift also hides its pages from the recent apps screen.

Does Sift track me?

Sift itself collects nothing about you and has no analytics of its own. Its ads come from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads, and Google's scanner and text recognition may send Google diagnostic data, never your pages or their text. The Privacy Policy has the details, and Privacy choices in Settings changes your ad consent where it applies.

Why does Sift need Google Play services?

Google's document scanner runs inside Google Play services, which downloads it once, usually in the background soon after you start using Sift. Without it you can still take a photo of a page with your camera app, or import a picture or PDF you already have.

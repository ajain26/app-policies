Where is my data stored?

Entirely on this device. There is no account, and nothing you write is sent to us.

I don't know my birth time. Can I still use AstroLife?

Yes. Without a time you get a solar chart: your Sun, Moon and planets are calculated as usual, and only your rising sign and houses are left out. You can add a time later in Settings, Your birth details.

My birthplace isn't in the list.

The list holds every city of about 15,000 people or more. Choose the nearest one; a few kilometres barely change a chart.

How do follow-ups work?

When you ask a question, the app finds the next date a slow planet makes an exact aspect to the part of your chart the question is about, and comes back to you in your thread on that date. Turn the notifications on or off in Settings.

Are the readings predictions or advice?

No. They are for entertainment and self-reflection. They do not predict events and are not medical, financial or legal advice.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep AstroLife free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on Today, Chart and People, and now and then a full screen ad appears when you close a forecast or a reading you finished. There are no ads in your thread. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

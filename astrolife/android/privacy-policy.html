Privacy Policy

AstroLife for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

AstroLife for Android ("the app") is an astrology app for entertainment and self-reflection, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your birth details, your chart and everything you write stay on this device and are never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

AstroLife for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What you enter stays on this device

Everything you enter is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: your name, birth date, birth time and birthplace, your answers during setup, the questions you ask in your thread and the readings written for them, your feedback on readings, messages to your future self, and the birth details of people you add for compatibility readings.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • Only add another person's birth details if you are comfortable doing so; like your own, they stay on this device.

3. How readings are made

Your chart, the planets' positions, transits, forecasts and compatibility are calculated on this device. Readings are composed on this device from a fixed set of interpretations written for each placement, aspect and transit. Your questions are not sent to any service to be answered.

Birthplaces are looked up in a list of cities built into the app (from GeoNames), so the place you type is never sent anywhere. The app does not use your device's location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

AstroLife is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Today, Chart and People tabs and, now and then, a full screen ad when you close a forecast, the months ahead, the Year Ahead or a compatibility reading after reading it. No ad is requested before you finish the setup steps, and no ad appears in your thread. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive what you entered: no birth detail, question, reading or message is passed to the ad service. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

6. Notifications and the widget

The daily reading note and the follow-ups on what you asked are planned and shown by this device. Nothing about them is sent to us. On a locked screen a follow-up hides the words of your question. The home screen widget shows today's reading from this device.

7. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include the app's saved data (your profile, thread, messages, people and settings) in that backup and restore it to a new device. On Android 12 and later the app allows this cloud backup only when the device can encrypt it end to end, which needs a screen lock. The same data can also be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. Your ad choices are not included.

8. Permissions

  • Notifications: for the daily reading and follow-ups. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Run at startup: so planned notifications survive a restart of your phone.
  • Internet and network state: for ads and Google's privacy message only.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

9. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

10. Children

AstroLife is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Use Reset everything in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; each removes everything the app stored on this device. Copies in a Google backup stay until you delete them there.

12. Security

Your information stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

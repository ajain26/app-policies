Where is my album stored?

Only on this device. Use Settings, Export the whole album, to keep a copy of everything, photographs and recordings included.

How do I move the album to a new phone?

Use Settings, Export the whole album, and copy the file to the new phone. Install Cairn there and open Settings, Restore from a file, then choose the file. Restoring replaces what is in Cairn on the new phone.

How do photographs get into the album?

Choose them with Android's photo picker, from the composer or from Look through at the bottom of a month. Each photograph is filed on the day it was taken, and Cairn keeps its own copy, so the album still works if you delete the original.

What is Look Back?

On days that have something behind them, such as the same day in an earlier year or the day they arrived, a small badge appears at the top of the album. Tap it to see those days again.

My reminder came late or did not come

Check that notifications are allowed for Cairn in your Android settings. To save battery, Android can deliver a reminder somewhat late. In These days, only the one evening note is sent, and after an animal has died the app sends nothing at all.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Cairn free, with no purchases. A banner sits above the tab bar on the Album and Life pages and in Settings, and now and then a full screen ad appears when you return to the album after saving a day. Ads never appear on the pages of an animal in Last Days or after they have died. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

Privacy Policy

Cairn for Android. Last updated: October 31, 2026

Cairn for Android ("the app") is a private photo album and diary for your dog or cat, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to the photographs, notes and recordings you keep in it and what the advertising in the app collects. In short: your album stays on this device and is never sent to us, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them.

1. Who we are

Cairn for Android is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. Your album stays on this device

Everything you keep in Cairn is stored only on this device, in the app's private storage: your animals' profiles, the days you write down, the photographs you add, the voice recordings you make, the Sunday Letters the app writes from your own week, the letters you seal for later, the Vault (weights, vet visits, vaccinations, medications and the comfort log) and your settings.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services of our own.
  • The Sunday Letter, the Life Book and the daily question are written on this device by the app's own templates from what you wrote. Nothing is sent anywhere to write them.

3. Photographs

Cairn uses Android's system photo picker. The app sees only the photographs you choose in it, never the rest of your library, and it does not ask for permission to read your photos. Each photograph you choose is copied into the app's private storage, together with the date it was taken, so the album keeps working if the original is deleted. The copies stay on this device.

4. Voice recordings

When you choose to record a voice note or the sound your animal makes, the app asks for the microphone and records only while the recording screen is open. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage on this device and are never sent to us.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cairn is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC: a banner above the tab bar on the Album and Life pages and in Settings and, now and then, a full screen ad when you return to the album after saving a day. Ads are never shown on the pages of an animal in Last Days or after they have died. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app launches and how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your album: no note, photograph, recording, letter, name or health entry is passed to the ad service. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Your ad choices

  • Ad privacy choices: where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on this device.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

7. Reminders

The evening reminder, medication reminders and the notes on days worth looking back at are scheduled and shown by this device, at the times you choose. They never quote what you wrote; they say no more than your animal's name. Nothing about them is sent to us.

8. Files you export or share

The archive you export from Settings holds your whole album: notes, letters, the Vault, recordings and the photographs you added. The Vault records, the comfort log, the Life Book and a Look Back card can also be shared as files. Each goes only where you choose, such as a folder on your device, an email or a cloud drive. Once a file leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled. When you restore an archive, the system file picker gives the app only the one file you choose.

9. Android backup and device transfer

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can include the text of your album (profiles, notes, letters and the Vault, without photographs or recordings, which are too large for Android's backup) in that backup and restore it to a new device, and the same text can be copied when you move to a new phone with a device to device transfer. Backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and you can turn backup off in your Android settings. To move everything, including photographs and recordings, use Export the whole album in Settings.

10. Permissions

  • Microphone: asked only when you start a recording. You can deny it, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Notifications: asked on the reminder step when you set up the app, or when you turn a reminder on. You can deny it, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads and Google's privacy message.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, photo library or files, and it does not use exact alarms.

11. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

12. Children

Cairn is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

13. Deleting your data

Your data stays on this device until you delete it. Use Erase everything in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; each removes everything the app stored on this device. Archives and files you saved elsewhere, and copies in a Google backup, stay until you delete them.

14. Security

Your album stays on this device, where it is protected by the device's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The app sends nothing over unencrypted connections.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new date.

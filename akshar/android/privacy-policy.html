Privacy Policy

Akshar for Android. Effective date: 4 November 2026

Akshar ("the app") is a Hindi word puzzle game for Android whose puzzles work offline, published on Google Play by Anshul Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your progress stays on your phone, there is no account, and the app is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, which collect some device information to serve them. The app also counts anonymous usage events with Firebase Analytics, with no personal data.

1. Who we are

Akshar is published on Google Play by Anshul Apps, an independent developer in India. You can reach us at anshul.jainsm@gmail.com.

2. What you do in the app stays on your phone

The app saves your progress only on your phone, in the app's private storage: finished puzzles, your streak, stats, the puzzle you are in the middle of, how many hints you have used today, and your settings and reminder time.

  • None of it is sent to us. We run no server that receives your data.
  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • All words, crosswords, levels and riddles are bundled inside the app, so the puzzles themselves need no internet. The internet is used only for ads, Google's privacy message and anonymous usage events, as described in sections 3, 4 and 5.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Akshar is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. A banner appears on the home, riddle list and progress screens. A full screen ad can appear at a break between puzzles, and a short video ad appears only if you choose to watch it to get a hint. No ad appears during a puzzle, at launch or at exit. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device or advertising identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID of this device
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version
  • app interactions, such as app launches and how you interact with ads, for example views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy use and crash data

Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ads never receive your puzzle progress. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown, which do not identify you.

How Google uses this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to learn how many people play and which parts they use, so we can improve the app. It records usage events such as: a puzzle was completed (which mode, and whether it was solved), a hint was used (free or after a video ad), and a puzzle or riddle was shared. It also records standard information Firebase collects automatically, such as app opens, the app version, Android version, device model, language and country, and an app instance identifier that is not your name or email. These events contain no personal data and never include what you typed. Firebase Analytics is run by Google and follows Google's privacy terms at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your choices

  • Where privacy laws require it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's privacy message (the User Messaging Platform consent tool) before personalized ads or analytics storage are enabled, and Settings then shows Privacy choices, where you can review or change your choice at any time. Google's consent tool stores your choice on your phone.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy, Ads; the exact place varies by device).
  • Without consent, or with personalized ads turned off, Google can still show ads that are not personalized.

6. Reminders are optional

The daily reminder is optional and is off until you turn it on, in Settings or when the app offers it after your first daily word. It is planned and shown by your phone, and nothing about it is sent to us. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications the first time you turn the reminder on; you can deny it, or change it later in Android settings.

7. Sharing puzzles

When you share a result or a riddle card, Android's share sheet sends it only to the app and the people you choose. Nothing is shared automatically. Once something leaves the app, the app or service you send it to decides how it is handled.

8. Reporting a problem with a puzzle

The report button in the app only opens your email app with a message you can read and edit before you send it. It names the puzzle and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix the puzzle, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

9. Android backup and a new phone

The app allows Android's own backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android can copy your saved progress to your Google Drive backup and restore it when you set up a new phone with the same account. This is done by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot see the backup. You can turn it off in your phone's Google settings.

10. Permissions

  • Notifications: for the daily reminder. You can deny this, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Internet and network state: only for ads, Firebase Analytics and Google's privacy message. Puzzles themselves work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob and Firebase as described above.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos or files.

11. Selling and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and analytics information that Google collects as described above, the app shares nothing with third parties.

12. Age

Akshar is meant for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Deleting your data

Your progress stays on your phone until you remove it. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app; either removes everything the app stored on your phone. If Android backup is on, delete the backup from your Google account settings. Emails you sent us are deleted on request.

14. Security

Your progress stays on your phone, where it is protected by the phone's own security. Keep a screen lock enabled. The ad and analytics services (Google's SDKs) control how they send data, and Google states that they encrypt it in transit.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

16. Contact

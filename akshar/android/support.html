Support

Akshar for Android. Effective date: 4 November 2026

Questions, ideas or a word that looks wrong? Email anshul.jainsm@gmail.com and I read everything. Akshar is made by Anshul Apps.

How do I play?

Akshar has four modes on the home screen: the daily word (a new word every day, India time), word connect, a mini crossword and riddles. Every tile is a whole akshara, so a letter with its matra is one tile. Colours understand matras: green means the right akshara in the right place, blue means the right letter with a different matra, and yellow means the akshara is in the word but elsewhere. The how to play page in Settings has examples.

Does it need internet?

No. All puzzles are inside the app and there is no sign in. Only ads and Google's privacy message use the internet.

A word was not accepted, or an answer looks wrong. How do I report it?

Use the report button on the puzzle. It opens your email app with a message that already names the puzzle and the app version; add what you think is wrong and send it. You can also write to anshul.jainsm@gmail.com. Confirmed errors are fixed in a later update.

How do hints work?

You get one free hint a day, shared across all modes. After that you can choose to watch a short video ad to get one more letter. If no ad is available the puzzle stays playable and nothing else is offered.

Where is my progress stored?

On your phone, saved after every move. There is no account. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android can restore your progress when you set up a new phone with the same account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling without a backup removes everything.

Why are there ads?

Ads from Google AdMob keep Akshar free. There is a banner on the home, riddle list and progress screens, and a full screen ad can appear at a break between puzzles. Ads never appear during a puzzle, at launch or at exit. Where your region requires consent, Settings shows Privacy choices so you can change your ad choices at any time.

How does the daily reminder work?

Turn it on in Settings and pick a time. On Android 13 and later, allow notifications when the app asks. If it does not arrive, check that notifications for Akshar are on in Android settings and that battery saving is not stopping the app. It can arrive a few minutes late.

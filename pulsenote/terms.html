What PulseNote does

PulseNote helps you log blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose, weight, oxygen saturation, water, and medications, review charts and history, set reminders, and estimate heart rate with your iPhone camera for fitness and wellness purposes.

Not a medical device

PulseNote is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. The camera does not measure blood pressure, blood glucose, or oxygen. Blood pressure categories, Heart Score, Heart Age, summaries, and answers describe your logged numbers and are general wellness information, not clinical assessments. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health, and never delay or disregard professional advice because of something you saw in the app.

Emergencies

If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, sudden numbness or weakness, or think you may be having a heart attack or stroke, call your local emergency number immediately. Do not wait for an app reading.

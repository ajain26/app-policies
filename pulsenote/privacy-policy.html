PulseNote

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Your records

PulseNote stores the readings, measurements, medication logs, wellness check-ins, and profile details you enter to show your history and insights. You can use the app without creating a PulseNote account. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and the developer does not collect your health data.

Local storage

PulseNote keeps your records on your device and excludes its health database from device backups. The app does not sync health records through iCloud. Export records you want to keep or share, and optionally save supported readings to Apple Health. Deleting the app or losing the device can remove records that you have not exported.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. You choose which types of data PulseNote may read and write in the system permission screen. Import runs only when you request it. Imported Health records are stored separately on your device, are excluded from device backups, and are not synced to iCloud by PulseNote. Removing an imported record from PulseNote does not delete the original from Apple Health. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties. You can change access in the Health app or in system Settings.

Camera measurements

The camera processes fingertip color changes on your device to estimate heart rate. PulseNote does not save or upload camera video. Saved measurements include the result and signal quality. Camera estimates are for wellness use and do not measure blood pressure.

Insights

Insights are calculated on your device from your logged data. PulseNote does not send your readings to a remote service for analysis. Heart Age, Heart Score, and other estimates are general wellness information and are not a diagnosis.

Widgets and notifications

Widgets share a small snapshot with the PulseNote widget extension on your device: your latest blood pressure and heart rate, the time of your latest record, and your logging streak. These values can come from readings you logged in PulseNote or from records you imported from Apple Health. While App Lock is enabled, PulseNote clears that snapshot and widgets hide your readings. When App Lock is off, widget readings may be visible on a locked screen. Reminder notifications use generic text without medication names, doses, or reading values. PulseNote removes older delivered notifications that contain private reminder details and keeps generic delivered reminders. You control widget placement and notification visibility in system settings.

Purchases and exports

Apple processes Premium purchases and manages your subscription. PulseNote uses the purchase status to unlock features and does not receive your payment card details. Reports and CSV files are created on your device; sharing them sends the included information to the recipient or app you choose.

Your controls

You can review, edit, or remove manual readings in History and remove imported records locally. Saved camera measurements and medication logs can also be deleted in the app. Turning off Apple Health sync stops future exports; records already saved in Apple Health remain under your control there. Face ID or your device passcode can protect access to the app when App Lock is enabled. Removing the device passcode turns App Lock off so you can still access your records.

Children

PulseNote is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.

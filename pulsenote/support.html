Common questions

The camera heart rate measurement keeps failing.

Cover both the rear camera lenses and the flash with one fingertip, press lightly, and keep still for the whole measurement. Cold fingers and strong ambient light make it harder. If your iPhone feels warm, let it cool for a few minutes first. A failed or cancelled measurement does not count toward the free daily limit.

Can PulseNote measure my blood pressure with the camera?

No. The camera only estimates heart rate. Log blood pressure from your cuff, or import it from Apple Health.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone?

PulseNote keeps records on your device and excludes them from backups. Turn on More > Save new logs to Apple Health, and your new logs are also saved in Apple Health. On the new iPhone, use More > Import from Apple Health to bring them back. You can also keep a copy with More > Export CSV.

I turned on App Lock and cannot get in.

App Lock uses Face ID or your device passcode. If you remove your device passcode, PulseNote turns App Lock off automatically so you can still reach your records.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PulseNote. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my records?

Swipe to delete readings in History, or delete saved measurements from their detail screen. Removing imported records in PulseNote does not delete them from Apple Health. Deleting the app removes all PulseNote records from your iPhone.

Is PulseNote a medical device?

No. PulseNote is a wellness log and does not diagnose or treat any condition. See the Terms of Use.
